Theranos promised revolutionary health technology, but a massive investigation completely shattered the nine-billion-dollar company. Founded by nineteen-year-old Elizabeth Holmes, Theranos falsely claimed its compact automated devices could run rapid blood tests using tiny amounts of blood. Investors poured in over seven hundred million dollars. Then came the turning point. In October 2015, John Carreyrou of The Wall Street Journal reported that Theranos secretly used traditional machines because its Edison devices provided inaccurate results. Regulators descended. CMS declared the Newark lab an immediate jeopardy to patient health, revoked its certificate, and issued sanctions. By June 2016, Forbes estimated Holmes's personal net worth dropped to nothing.
