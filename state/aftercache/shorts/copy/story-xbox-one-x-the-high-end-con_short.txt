How the Xbox One X Sank Its Own Sales. First revealed under the codename Project Scorpio, the hardware was built to run games at native 4K resolution. Microsoft even showcased Halo 5 running without dynamic scaling. It officially launched on November 7, 2017. The 1 TB model debuted at a steep US$499 price tag, alongside a limited Project Scorpio Edition. Yet, hardware dominance was not enough. By the end of 2020, production of all Xbox One consoles ceased completely as the new Xbox Series X and Series S launched. Its massive power could not save it.
