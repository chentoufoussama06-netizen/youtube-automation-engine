How Bebo went from $850 million sale to near extinction? Bebo, the social networking site founded by Michael and Xochi Birch in January 2005, surged to prominence by overtaking MySpace as the United Kingdom’s most‑used platform, amassing at least 10. 7 million unique users. In March 2008 AOL purchased Bebo for $850 million, a deal later condemned as one of the dot‑com era’s worst, and the site began to lose ground as Facebook’s mobile‑first strategy attracted its audience. By April 2010 AOL announced plans to sell or shut down Bebo, citing dwindling user numbers and an inability to compete in its existing form.
