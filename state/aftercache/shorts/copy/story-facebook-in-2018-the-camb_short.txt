Facebook shocked the world in 2018 when the Cambridge Analytica scandal exploded, exposing data harvested from millions of users without consent. The breach began when Global Science Research sold information on over 87 million Facebook profiles to the political data firm led by Alexander Nix. An app used by just 270,000 people let the API pull friends’ data secretly. Facebook first downplayed the breach, then announced the suspension of Cambridge Analytica amid mounting criticism. The fallout reminded everyone that even a “social” network can become a data mine.
