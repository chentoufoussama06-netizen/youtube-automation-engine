In the 1990s, Netscape Navigator dominated the web, but Microsoft fought back to crush its main rival. Netscape created the flagship web browser, making it the market leader. Then Microsoft released Internet Explorer. To defeat Netscape, Microsoft bundled Internet Explorer with the Windows operating system and forced PC vendors to distribute it. By 1999, Internet Explorer 5. 0 took significant market share, and by 2003 Netscape's user base had all but disappeared. A court later ruled Microsoft’s bundling was an illegal monopolistic practice, but it came too late. AOL officially canceled Netscape Navigator on December 28, 2007. The web titan was completely dethroned.
