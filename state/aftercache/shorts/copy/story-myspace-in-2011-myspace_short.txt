Myspace once fell from a massive twelve billion dollar valuation to selling for just thirty-five million dollars. News Corporation attempted to merge the giant social network with Yahoo! in 2007. At its peak, the site held incredible power. But heavy advertising slowed the platform down, and Facebook eventually surpassed it in unique United States visitors. By late February 2011, News Corporation officially put the site up for sale. Heavy user losses deters suitors, and a deadline passed without bids meeting the one hundred million dollar reserve price. In June 2011, Specific Media Group and Justin Timberlake jointly purchased Myspace for approximately thirty-five million dollars. Rupert Murdoch called the purchase a huge mistake.
