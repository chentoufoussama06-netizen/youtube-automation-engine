Fountain pens leaked at altitude. Royal Air Force aircrew needed something better. In 1945, the UK adopted László Bíró’s ballpoint pen. Earlier ballpoints failed because ink overflowed or clogged. László Bíró coupled viscous ink with a ball-and-socket mechanism for controlled flow. British engineer Frederick George Miles licensed the design. Manufactured by Miles Aircraft as the Biro, it proved far more versatile in airplanes than leaking fountain pens.
