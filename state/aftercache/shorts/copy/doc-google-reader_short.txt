Google shut down Reader—why did Feedly gain 500,000 users in 48 hours? Google Reader, the RSS and Atom feed aggregator launched by Google on 7 October 2005, was announced for discontinuation on 13 March 2013. Google explained that the service had a loyal but declining following and that the company wanted to concentrate on fewer products, giving users until 1 July 2013 to export their data via Google Takeout. The news sparked an immediate wave of protest. Within 48 hours, Feedly reported more than 500,000 new registrations, and over the next two weeks the platform added three million users. NewsBlur’s subscriber count jumped from roughly 1,500 to over 60,000.
