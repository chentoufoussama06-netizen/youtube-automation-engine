Paracord was originally used in the suspension lines of parachutes. After World War II, this lightweight nylon rope became available to civilians as military surplus and common retail products from various surplus stores and websites. Hikers and outdoor sports enthusiasts used survival bracelets made of several feet of paracord woven into a compact and wearable form. Astronauts even used it during the 82nd Space Shuttle mission to repair the Hubble Space Telescope.
