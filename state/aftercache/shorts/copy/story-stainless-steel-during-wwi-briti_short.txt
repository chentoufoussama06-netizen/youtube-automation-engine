Harry Brearley searched for a corrosion-resistant alloy. In 1913, while seeking a material for gun barrels in Sheffield, England, he discovered and subsequently industrialized a martensitic stainless steel alloy. He worked with a local cutlery manufacturer who gave it the name stainless steel. The discovery was announced two years later in a newspaper article in The New York Times.
