Vine was launched in 2013 and became a massive phenomenon, but Twitter shut it down just four years later. Founded by Dom Hofmann, Rus Yusupov, and Colin Kroll, Vine allowed users to share six-second looping video clips. Twitter bought the company for $30 million before its launch on January 24, 2013. By April, Vine became the most downloaded free iOS app. It launched viral comedy, music trends, and iconic memes. Then Twitter abruptly disabled uploads on October 27, 2016. On January 17, 2017, Twitter officially shut Vine down, ending the beloved platform. An entire viral era was gone forever.
