In 2008, Bebo sold for $850 million, but just five years later, it collapsed to a million-dollar bargain. Founded in January 2005 by Michael and Xochi Birch, the social network became the UK's most used platform, registering over 10. AOL bought the site in March 2008. Then, users fled to Facebook. AOL sold Bebo to Criterion Capital Partners in 2010, and by May 2013, the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. On July 1, 2013, the original founders bought Bebo back for just $1 million. They deleted all user content and eventually shut it down.
