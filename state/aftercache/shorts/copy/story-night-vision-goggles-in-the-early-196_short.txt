1,000-fold. That is how much night-vision goggles amplified light. The US Army developed these passive devices in the 1960s. They relied on ambient light instead of using an extra infrared light source. Using an S-20 photocathode, their image intensifiers amplified light around 1,000-fold. They were quite bulky and required moonlight to function properly. Today, night vision devices are available to civilian users for applications including aviation, driving, and demining.
