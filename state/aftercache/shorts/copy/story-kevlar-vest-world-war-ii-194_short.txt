Kevlar Vests Were First Made for WWII Soldiers. Early US designs for body armor were far too heavy for infantrymen to wear. Near the middle of 1944, development restarted. The US developed a vest using fiberglass-based plastic called doron plate. These new vests finally reached troops for the Battle of Okinawa in 1945.
