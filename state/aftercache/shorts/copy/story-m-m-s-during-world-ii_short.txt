M&M's were invented so soldiers could eat chocolate that wouldn't melt. saw soldiers eating candy in the Spanish Civil War. Their chocolate had a hard sugar shell so it would not melt in warm climates. He patented his own process in the United States in 1941 to create M&M's. Army became his first big customer. During World War II, the non-melting chocolates were sold exclusively to the military.
