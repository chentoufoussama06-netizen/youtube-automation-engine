Ten patients. That was all the US penicillin available by June 1942. Infected wounds threatened Allied forces fighting in World War II. The War Production Board drew up a plan for the mass distribution of penicillin stocks to troops in Europe. A mouldy cantaloupe in Illinois was found to contain the best strain for production. Mass production resulted from a deep-tank fermentation plant. Lives were saved.
