Zippo lighters were made for WWII soldiers. The company stopped production for consumer markets and dedicated all manufacturing to the US military. During those war years, metal shortages forced a change from brass to a black crackle finished steel. American troops depended on it so heavily that Ernie Pyle wrote that Zippo was probably the most important element on the front.
