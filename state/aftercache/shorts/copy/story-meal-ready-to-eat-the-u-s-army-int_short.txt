Meal, Ready-to-Eat arrived in 1981, ending heavy canned rations. Could soldiers finally eat without a pot or fire? The Department of Defense began the program in 1963, produced the Long Range Patrol pack in 1966, and Abdul Rahman created early freeze-dried prototypes. Dr. Rauno Lampi refined a retort pouch that required no water, leading to an issue that year and issue by 1986. Did it work?
