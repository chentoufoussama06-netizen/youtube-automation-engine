How did RapidShare go from 3 million users to a single employee? RapidShare opened in 2002 as a modest file‑hosting service founded by Christian Schmid in Mülheim, Germany, and by 2009 it ranked among the Internet’s 20 most visited sites, claiming ten petabytes of user‑uploaded files and the capacity to serve three million users simultaneously. The platform offered unique download URLs, allowing anyone with a link to retrieve a file, while forbidding public searches of its servers. After the 2012 takedown of Megaupload, lawsuits from copyright owners forced RapidShare to abandon its free, anonymous sharing model and pivot to a subscription‑only, B2B cloud‑storage service.
