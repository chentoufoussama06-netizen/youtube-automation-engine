Karl Bushby attempted to walk around the world with unbroken footsteps, facing danger at every turn. In November 1998, the former paratrooper set off from Punta Arenas, Chile, aiming for his home town in England without motorized transport. Bushby braved the inhospitable Darién Gap, evading Colombian FARC guerrillas, only to be jailed in Panama for lacking a visa. In 2006, he trekked across the frozen Bering Strait into Russia, where border guards detained him. Russian visa bans and Chinese detention caused years of delays. He finally reached England in 2026. His 12-year journey took 28 relentless years.
