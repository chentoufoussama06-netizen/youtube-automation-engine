Jonestown was a remote commune in Guyana where over 900 people met a horrific, deadly end. Led by Jim Jones, the Peoples Temple built the settlement to escape media investigations of abuse. Tensions escalated in November 1978. First, Jones ordered Temple members to murder five people at a nearby airstrip, including U. Then, Jones directed a mass poison event. On an audio tape, he called it a revolutionary suicide. While Christine Miller argued against it, others pushed back. In total, 918 people died across three locations. Many were injected with cyanide against their will. It remains an infamous mass murder-suicide.
