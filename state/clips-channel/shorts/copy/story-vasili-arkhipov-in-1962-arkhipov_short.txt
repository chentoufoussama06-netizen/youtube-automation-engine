In 1962, Vasili Arkhipov stood between humanity and total nuclear destruction during the Cuban Missile Crisis. American destroyers dropped practice depth charges to force the submarine to surface. Trapped underwater without contact from Moscow, the crew believed war had already begun. Captain Valentin Savitsky and Political Officer Ivan Maslennikov prepared to launch a nuclear T-5 torpedo. Authorization required all three senior officers to agree. Arkhipov refused. He persuaded Savitsky to surface instead. Vasili Arkhipov single-handedly prevented nuclear war and saved the world.
