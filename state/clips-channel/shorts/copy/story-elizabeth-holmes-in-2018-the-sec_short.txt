In 2018, Elizabeth Holmes faced a massive fraud charge that shattered her health technology company, Theranos. Holmes raised over $700 million from investors by claiming her technology revolutionized blood testing with small fingerprick volumes. In reality, she lied about the accuracy of her devices, falsely claimed military use, and lied about a $100 million revenue stream when making only $100,000. The Securities and Exchange Commission charged Holmes and former president Ramesh Balwani with fraud. Holmes settled the charges, surrendering her voting control and accepting a ten-year ban from serving as a public company officer. Her multi-billion-dollar empire completely collapsed.
