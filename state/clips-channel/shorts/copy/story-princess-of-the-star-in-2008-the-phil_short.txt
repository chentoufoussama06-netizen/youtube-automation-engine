814 people. That is how many died on Princess of the Stars. MV Princess of the Stars sailed from Manila en route to Cebu City despite the storm. The huge ferry unexpectedly faced the typhoon when the storm changed course. Rough seas overwhelmed the vessel off the coast of Romblon. Passengers put on life jackets before the captain gave the order to abandon ship. The ship capsized, leaving eight hundred fourteen people dead as a result of the disaster.
