At eight minutes past midnight, a luxury steamship crashed into steep cliffs. SS Wairarapa was speeding through thick fog off the coast of New Zealand. Captain John McIntosh refused to slow down from nearly full speed. The ship went fatally off-course. Rough seas swallowed non-swimmers, and around 130 people died in the tragedy. It took three full days for news of the disaster to reach Auckland.
