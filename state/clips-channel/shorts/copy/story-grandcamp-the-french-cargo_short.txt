Texas City burned after a mid-morning fire broke out on board Grandcamp. The French-registered vessel carried a cargo of about 2,300 short tons of ammonium nitrate. Captain Guillebon ordered hatches sealed and steam pumped inside to smother the flames. A massive explosion blew thousands of tons of steel into the air, killing at least 581 people in the deadliest industrial accident in the history of the United States.
