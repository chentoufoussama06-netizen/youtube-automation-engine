122. That is how many people died when SS Yongala vanished. A cyclone approached off Queensland before she left the sight of land. The ship sailed into the night and vanished completely. Searchers found wreckage washing ashore, but no trace of the passengers or crew. Decades passed with no answers. Then, in 1958, a local fisherman finally discovered the lost wreck on the sea floor.
