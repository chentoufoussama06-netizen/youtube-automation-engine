"Attempting to blow." Those garbled words were the final transmission from USS Thresher. On 10 April 1963, the US Navy's fastest and quietest nuclear submarine plunged into deep-dive trials off Massachusetts. Under Lieutenant Commander John Wesley Harvey, she descended near her test depth. Then, silence. Thresher sank 350 km east of Cape Cod, taking all 129 people aboard. She remains on Eternal Patrol.
