343. That is how many people died when Princess Sophia sank. The ship drifted off course in Lynn Canal. At two in the morning, Princess Sophia struck ground hard on Vanderbilt Reef. Rescue vessels gathered around. Worsening weather forced them away as the storm raged. Electrical power failed. The final wireless message arrived. The ship foundered. No one survived the disaster.
