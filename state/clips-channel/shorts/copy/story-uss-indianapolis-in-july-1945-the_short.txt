Days after delivering a secret atomic bomb, USS Indianapolis was torpedoed. What fate awaited the 890 men left drifting after the secret bomb run? At 0015 on 30 July 1945 Japanese submarine I-58 launched two torpedoes; the cruiser listed, rolled, and vanished. Of 1,195 crew, about 300 went down, and only 316 of the 890 adrift survived the sharks, exposure and thirst. Survive it?
