Three torpedoes. That is what destroyed the Titanic's famous rescuer, RMS Carpathia. Six years later, she sailed as a commodore ship in a convoy. On seventeen July nineteen eighteen, a German submarine sighted her in the Southwest Approaches and fired three torpedoes. The explosions destroyed her engines and electrical gear. Captain William Prothero ordered everyone to abandon ship. Five crew members lost their lives before HMS Snowdrop drove away the submarine.
