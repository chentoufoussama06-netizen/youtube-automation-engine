Fletcher Christian seized the British ship Bounty in a bloodless mutiny. Commanding officer William Bligh was set adrift in a launch with his loyalists. Hoping to elude the Royal Navy, Christian gathered crew members and kidnapped Tahitian men and women. They sailed to remote Pitcairn Island to hide. To prevent their detection and any escape, the mutineers burned Bounty on 23 January 1790 in what is now called Bounty Bay.
