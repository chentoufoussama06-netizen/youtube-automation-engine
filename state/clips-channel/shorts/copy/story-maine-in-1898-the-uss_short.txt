Two hundred and sixty-eight sailors died when the USS Maine exploded. Sent to Havana Harbor to protect the US, the ship sank on the evening of February 15, 1898. Newspapers claimed the Spanish were responsible. The phrase "Remember the Maine! To hell with Spain!" became a rallying cry. Evidence points to an internal coal bunker fire. The Spanish-American War began on April 21, 1898.
