306 souls vanished aboard USS Cyclops without a single piece of wreckage. The Navy cargo ship left Brazil carrying manganese ore toward Baltimore. Commander Worley reported a cracked cylinder. Barbados officials noted the ship sat too low in the water. Then she disappeared without a trace. Assistant Secretary of the Navy Franklin D. Roosevelt officially declared the ship lost. No wreckage was ever found. Theories range from structural failure to the Bermuda Triangle.
