Mont-Blanc loomed in Halifax Harbour, a powder keg waiting. What would happen if a Norwegian freighter struck her? At 8:45 am on December 6, 1917, SS Imo collided with Mont-Blanc. Benzole spilled, sparks ignited vapours, and a fire erupted. By 9:04:35 am cargo—2,300 tons of picric acid, 500 tons of TNT, 10 tons of guncotton—detonated. The blast killed about 2,000 people and injured 9,000. Who bears the ultimate blame for that deadly collision?
