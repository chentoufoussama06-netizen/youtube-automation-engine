30,000 pounds of gold. That is what sank with SS Central America. The sidewheel steamer carried hundreds of passengers toward New York City. A Category 2 hurricane struck off the coast of the Carolinas. Winds reached 105 mph and shredded her sails. Water flooded the ship as her boiler failed. Bilge pumps stopped working and the vessel sank. Over 400 lives were lost in the deep blue sea.
