Less than twenty minutes. That is all it took for General Slocum to collapse. On June 15, 1904, the passenger steamboat caught fire in the East River of New York City. The ship carried members of St. Mark's Evangelical Lutheran Church to a church picnic. Nearly 1,400 passengers boarded, mostly women and children. An estimated 1,021 people died in the disaster.
