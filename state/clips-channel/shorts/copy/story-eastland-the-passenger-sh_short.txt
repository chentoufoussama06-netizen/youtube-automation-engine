Eastland capsized on a Chicago dock, trapping hundreds inside. What began as a routine picnic turned deadly. At 6:30 a. , passengers boarded, and by 7:10 the ship hit its 2,500 limit. The vessel lurched, listed to port, and at 7:28 rolled onto its side. Half the hull stayed above water, but 841 passengers and two crew members drowned. Could stricter safety laws have prevented this tragedy?
