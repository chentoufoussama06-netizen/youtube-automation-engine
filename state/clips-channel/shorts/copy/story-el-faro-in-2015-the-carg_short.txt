33. That is how many crew members sank with El Faro. The cargo ship sailed directly toward the storm from Jacksonville, Florida, bound for Puerto Rico with thirty-three crew members on board. Water flooded a cargo hold and the ship lost its propulsion plant. The captain failed to take sufficient action to avoid the hurricane, and the vessel sank on October 1, 2015, with no survivors.
