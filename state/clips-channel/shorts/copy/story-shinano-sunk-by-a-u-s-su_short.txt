10 days. That is how long the gigantic Shinano survived. Shinano carried fifty rocket-propelled flying bombs toward Kure. Unfinished watertight doors and missing pumps left her vulnerable. On 29 November 1944, the submarine Archerfish fired six torpedoes into the ship. The massive vessel sank ten days after commissioning. She remains the largest ship ever sunk by a submarine.
