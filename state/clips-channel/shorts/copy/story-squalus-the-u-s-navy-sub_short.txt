243 feet. That is how deep Squalus bottomed during a test dive. Failure of the main induction valve flooded the engine rooms and crew quarters during a test dive. The submarine bottomed in deep water. A daring rescue operation used the new McCann Rescue Chamber for the first time. Thirty-three survivors were pulled to safety, marking a pioneering triumph in deep-sea rescue history.
