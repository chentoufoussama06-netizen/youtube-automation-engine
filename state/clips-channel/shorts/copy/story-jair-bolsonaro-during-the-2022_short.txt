Jair Bolsonaro survived a dramatic assassination attempt on 6 September during his 2018 presidential campaign. The former military officer was running for the presidency of Brazil. Everything was on the line as the country prepared for the first-round vote. While campaigning, an attacker attempted to take his life. Bolsonaro survived the deadly attack. He led the first-round results on 7 October. He took office as Brazil's 38th president on 1 January 2019. Survival paved his way to supreme power.
