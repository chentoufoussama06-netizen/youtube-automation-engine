HMS Audacious was the largest warship ever sunk by naval mines. Captain Cecil Dampier attempted to tow the battleship to safety. As water flooded the hull, the crew abandoned ship with help from nearby vessels. British authorities desperately tried to hide the disaster from the public. American tourists aboard a rescue ship recorded the sinking. The Admiralty embargoed the news in the UK. She sank anyway.
