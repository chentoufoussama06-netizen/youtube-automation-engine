Twenty-five people were trapped on the Arctic pack ice. The Canadian government had acquired Karluk as flagship for an expedition. In August 1913, the ship became locked in ice, drifting helplessly for months. In January 1914, the hull crushed and sank. The survivors faced a brutal march toward Wrangel Island. Eight lost their lives on the frozen sea, and three more died on the island before rescue arrived.
