Emden faced a relentless barrage from the Australian cruiser HMAS Sydney. The German warship suffered massive damage during the fierce battle near the Cocos Islands. Karl von Müller ran his ship aground on a reef to save the surviving crew. One hundred thirty-three men died in the engagement. The remaining crew members were captured the next day. The battered wreck was eventually broken up for scrap.
