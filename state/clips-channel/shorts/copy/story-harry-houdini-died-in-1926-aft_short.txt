Harry Houdini faced his ultimate tragedy in 1926 after a sudden dressing room encounter. On October 22, McGill University student J. Gordon Whitehead visited the performer at Montreal's Princess Theatre. Whitehead asked if punches to the stomach truly did not hurt him, then delivered forceful, hammer-like blows to Houdini's abdomen while he reclined on a couch with a broken ankle. Houdini suffered severe pain, ignored medical advice for acute appendicitis, and continued performing. He even passed out during his final show at Detroit's Garrick Theater. On October 31, 1926, Houdini died from peritonitis. The legendary magician was 52 years old.
