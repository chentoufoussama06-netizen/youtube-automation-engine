Two hundred and eleven people vanished without leaving a single trace. In July 1909, the British liner Waratah set out from Durban for Cape Town on her second voyage. The ship lacked radio equipment, leaving her completely cut off beyond visual range. When she failed to reach Cape Town, searchers scrambled. Royal Navy cruisers searched for weeks, and one even warped its hull in massive waves. The cargo ship Sabine searched twenty-six thousand kilometers across the ocean, but found nothing. Relatives raised funds for a final expedition that landed on uninhabited islands, yet yielded no clues. Waratah was posted missing at sea, her fate forever unknown.
