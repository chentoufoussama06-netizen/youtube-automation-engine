The USS Indianapolis sank in twelve minutes after finishing her top-secret mission. The heavy cruiser had just delivered uranium components for the Little Boy atomic bomb to Tinian. Now, she sank in just 12 minutes. Eight hundred and ninety men were set adrift in the ocean. Survivors faced salt water, hot sun, severe dehydration, and shark attacks. Only 316 of them survived the ordeal.
