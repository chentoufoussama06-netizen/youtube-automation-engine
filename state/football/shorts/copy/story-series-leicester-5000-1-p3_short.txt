Leicester City pushed past every expectation, moving within touching distance of the ultimate Premier League fairy tale. On 2 May 2016, the entire title race hinged on a fierce London derby at Stamford Bridge between Chelsea and Tottenham Hotspur. Tottenham needed a victory to stay in contention. In the first half, Harry Kane and Son Heung-min scored to put Tottenham 2–0 ahead. Chelsea fought back after the break, with Gary Cahill pulling one back. Then, substitute Eden Hazard scored a late equaliser. The match ended in a 2–2 draw, crushing Tottenham's hopes. At the full-time whistle, 5000–1 underdogs Leicester City were confirmed as champions of England for the first time in their history. Less than a year later, the man who made it happen was dismissed.
