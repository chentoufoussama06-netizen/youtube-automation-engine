Could a teenager carry Spain all the way to European glory? In Berlin, Luis de la Fuente’s side faced England in the Euro 2024 final. Cole Palmer scored from outside the box in the 73rd minute to equalise. Spain took the ball back in the midfield in the 82nd minute. Then, in the 86th minute, Mikel Oyarzabal outpaced his marker to score from a Marc Cucurella cross with his right foot. In the 90th minute, Marc Guéhi headed over Unai Simón toward goal. Dani Olmo stood on the line and headed it away. Part five is next.
