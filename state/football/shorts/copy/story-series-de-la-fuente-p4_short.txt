Spain stood ninety minutes away from absolute perfection. After conquering their group and surviving chaotic knockouts, Luis de la Fuente’s men marched into Berlin on 14 July 2024 for the Euro final against England. Both sides had conceded in every knockout game, setting up a tense clash in front of 65,600 fans. French referee François Letexier, at just 35, became the youngest referee in Euro final history as he blew the whistle to start the battle. Spain fought for a record-breaking fourth European Championship, while England aimed to avoid losing back-to-back finals. De la Fuente’s squad pushed relentlessly, completing a flawless run of seven wins in seven matches. Spain claimed the trophy with a 2–1 victory, setting a new tournament record of fifteen goals scored. Part five is next.
