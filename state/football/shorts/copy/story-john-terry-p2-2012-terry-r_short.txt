England captain quits after FA ban. New England manager Roy Hodgson selected Terry for UEFA Euro 2012, leaving out Rio Ferdinand to avoid conflict before Terry's trial for racially abusing Anton Ferdinand. Terry played all ninety minutes in England's four matches at the Euros. In July, a court acquitted Terry of criminal charges. But on July 27th, 2012, the FA charged him. Terry denied using abusive words referring to race. With his position untenable, Terry announced his international retirement on September 23rd, 2012. Four days later, the FA found him guilty, issuing a four-match ban and a two-hundred-and-twenty-thousand-pound fine.
