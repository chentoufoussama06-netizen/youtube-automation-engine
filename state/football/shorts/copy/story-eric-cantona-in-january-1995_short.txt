Eric Cantona’s KungFu Kick on Fan in 1995. It happened on 25 January 1995, an away match at Crystal Palace when United were chasing a third straight league title. Cantona had already scored the winning goal in a 1-0 home win over Blackburn on 22 January, bringing his league tally to twelve, and had added a FA-Cup strike at Sheffield United earlier that month. During the Palace game, referee Alan Wilkie sent him off for kicking defender Richard Shaw after a hard-working marking battle. As Cantona trudged toward the tunnel, Palace supporter Matthew Simmons rushed down eleven rows, shouting abuse. Cantona launched a flying “kung-fu” kick into the crowd, then threw a series of punches. He pleaded guilty to assault on 23 March, received a two-week prison sentence that was later reduced to 120 hours of community service, and the FA imposed an eight-month ban ending 30 September 1995. United fined him £30 000, stripped his captaincy and he missed Euro 96.
