Juventus star Edgar Davids was suddenly suspended for testing positive for steroids. Nicknamed "The Pitbull" for his aggression and hard tackling, the Dutch defensive midfielder was a recognizable star for Juventus, wearing protective goggles due to glaucoma. Then came May 17, 2001. FIFA dropped the hammer. Davids tested positive for the banned anabolic steroid, nandrolone. The suspension halted the star midfielder in his tracks. One of the generation's defining players suddenly found himself on the sidelines, punished by football's governing body. The ban silenced his engine room.
