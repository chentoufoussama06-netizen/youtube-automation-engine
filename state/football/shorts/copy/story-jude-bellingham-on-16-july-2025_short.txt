Three weeks after dislocating his shoulder, Jude Bellingham returned to action. Since dislocating his left shoulder in November 2023, the midfielder faced an ongoing battle while performing for Real Madrid. On 16 July 2025, Real Madrid confirmed he had successfully undergone surgery to address the long-standing issue. Reports expected him to be sidelined for approximately 10 to 12 weeks, missing the start of the 2025–26 La Liga season and the early stages of the Champions League group phase for an October return. But Bellingham returned early, playing in a 2–0 win over Espanyol on 20 September. He then scored match-winners against Juventus and Barcelona. Surgery could not slow him down.
