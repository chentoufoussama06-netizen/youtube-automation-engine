Critics claimed Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior failed to reach his expected heights. In a 2025 interview, the Brazilian star faced these accusations head-on. Neymar admitted that many things happened, stating, "Many things happened, I had many injuries that cost me a lot in my career". His years at Paris Saint-Germain were largely affected by injuries. Later at Al-Hilal, a hamstring injury sidelined him, leading coach Jorge Jesus to state Neymar could no longer perform at the level they were used to. Yet, Neymar defended his legacy. He expressed that he achieved almost everything he dreamed of, and even things he never dreamed of. Ultimately, he remained Brazil's all-time top goalscorer with 80 goals.
