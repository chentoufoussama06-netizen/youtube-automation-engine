An alleged sexual assault at a Barcelona nightclub changed everything. Dani Alves, one of football's most decorated players, faced charges after an incident on 30 December 2022. The complainant filed an official complaint on 2 January 2023. She alleged that Alves forced her into a bathroom, slapped her, and raped her. Investigators found matching DNA and fingerprints, while Alves gave five different accounts, initially denying he knew her before claiming consensual sex. Mexican club UNAM terminated his contract, and Catalan police arrested him without bail. His initial conviction was later overturned, and he was acquitted.
