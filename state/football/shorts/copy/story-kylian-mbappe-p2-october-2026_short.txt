Mbappé apologises, Chinese sponsors cut ties. The next day, the Real Madrid star issued a statement saying he “has deep respect for the Chinese people and their history and is sincerely sorry.” Real Madrid and La Liga quickly released their own distancing statements. Chinese brands Eastroc Beverage, Mengniu Dairy and Milkground terminated their contracts with him, and China Central Television suspended La Liga matches. The foreign ministry warned that any words hurting Chinese sentiment would meet opposition. In a single week, Mbappé’s career-defining reputation collided with a massive commercial fallout. The price of a joke proved priceless.
