Grande Torino, Italy’s invincible squad, vanished in a single tragic moment. On 4 May a Fiat G. 212 of Avio Linee Italiane, registration I-ELCE, took off at 09:40, stopped in Barcelona, then left for Turin at 14:50. Poor weather blanketed the city: clouds, showers, strong southwest gusts and visibility of only 40 metres. At 16:59 the pilot reported “Quota 2,000 m… we will cut at Superga.” At 17:03, turning left for landing, the aircraft slammed into the back of the Basilica of Superga’s retaining wall. All thirty-one aboard died, including every active Torino player, manager Ernő Egri Erbstein, coach Leslie Lievesley and three leading journalists.
