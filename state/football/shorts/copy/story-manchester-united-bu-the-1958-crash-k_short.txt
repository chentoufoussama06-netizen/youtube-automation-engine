Twenty-three people died after a horrific crash at Munich-Riem Airport. The Manchester United football team, nicknamed the "Busby Babes", were flying back from a quarter-final match against Red Star Belgrade. Their aircraft crashed on its third attempt to take off on 6 February 1958. Twenty people died at the scene, including seven Manchester United players. Among the injured was young prodigy Duncan Edwards, who tragically died in hospital 15 days later. Seriously injured manager Matt Busby fought for his life and received the Last Rites twice. The disaster claimed eight players in total, forever scarring the club.
