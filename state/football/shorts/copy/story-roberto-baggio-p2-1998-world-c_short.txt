Roberto Baggio stepped onto the field in France, the memory of 1994 still burning. Could he finally erase the miss that haunted him? In Italy’s opening match against Chile, Vieri opened the scoring from a Baggio assist, then Chile surged ahead through Marcelo Salas. Near the final whistle Baggio slipped a low cross that brushed Ronald Fuentes’s hand, awarding Italy a penalty. Despite the 1994 heartbreak, Baggio volunteered, struck the spot, and netted Italy’s equaliser – his first penalty for the Azzurri since the Rose Bowl. The goal lifted the team, but the tournament would end in another shoot-out loss. Redemption flickered, then faded.
