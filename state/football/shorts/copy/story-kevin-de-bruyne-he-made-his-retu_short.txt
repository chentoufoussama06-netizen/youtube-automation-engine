Four to five months out. That was the sentence handed down to Kevin De Bruyne after a hamstring injury struck him in the opening league game of the season against Burnley. Months of grueling recovery followed. He worked in the shadows, waiting for his chance to return to the pitch. Then came 7 January 2024. The stage was the FA Cup third round. Manchester City faced Huddersfield Town. De Bruyne made his long-awaited return from injury, stepping back onto the grass when his team needed him. He wasted no time making his presence felt. Providing an assist, he helped orchestrate a dominant 5–0 victory. The maestro was back.
