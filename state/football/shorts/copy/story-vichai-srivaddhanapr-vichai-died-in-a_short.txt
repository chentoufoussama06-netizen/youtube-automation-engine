Leicester City's 5000/1 title-winning owner died in a fiery stadium helicopter crash. Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha owned the Premier League team Leicester City. He bought the club in August 2010. Against 5000/1 odds, Leicester won the 2015–16 Premier League title. On 27 October 2018, his AgustaWestland AW169 helicopter took off from the pitch. Eyewitnesses saw the aircraft spinning before crashing and creating a fireball. The next day, it was confirmed that Vichai died in the crash alongside the pilots and two other passengers. Numerous tributes were laid outside the stadium by players and fans. Vichai is fondly remembered by Leicester supporters.
