Kane's World Cup heartbreak and bronze triumph. The captain was hunting for international glory. In the semi-final against Argentina, Kane earned his 121st international cap, becoming England’s most capped outfield player. But the match ended in heartbreak with a tight 2–1 defeat. Days later, Kane watched from the bench as an unused substitute while England defeated France 6–4 in a thrilling bronze medal match. England secured third place—their highest World Cup finish in 60 years. Kane left the tournament with a medal, taking his country to heights unseen since 1966.
