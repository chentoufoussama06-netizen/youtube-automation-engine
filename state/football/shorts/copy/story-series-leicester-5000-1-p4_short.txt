Claudio Ranieri took Leicester City from 5000–1 outsiders to the most unlikely champions in sporting history. He even won the Best FIFA Men's Coach award on 9 January 2017. But a brutal league slump followed. By late November, the champions sat in 14th place, just two points above the relegation zone. Then came a 2–1 Champions League loss to Sevilla. Senior players allegedly met with chairman Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha, and on 23 February 2017, Leicester dismissed Ranieri with the club one point above the drop zone. Gary Lineker called the panic decision "wrong" and shed a tear. In less than ten months, the fairytale ended, leaving the legendary Italian manager cast aside by the club he made famous.
