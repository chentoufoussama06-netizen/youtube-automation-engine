Following the death of his close friend Daniel Jarque, Andrés Iniesta suffered privately from episodes of psychological instability. Even when physically fit, he was often unable to complete training sessions or exert himself. He sought psychological help, including from psychologist Inma Puig. His season was fractured by these struggles and recurrent injuries. Then came the 2010 FIFA World Cup final against the Netherlands. In the 116th minute, Iniesta scored Spain's match-winning goal. He removed his jersey during his ecstatic goal celebration, earning a yellow card, and revealed the message "Dani Jarque, always with us" written on his undershirt, dedicating the moment to his late friend. It was the most important moment in his career.
