Real Madrid faced an eighteen-year Copa del Rey drought. José Mourinho took his team to the Mestalla in Valencia to face arch-rivals Barcelona. The two Spanish giants battled in a high-stakes final on 20 April 2011. Mourinho sought his very first trophy in Spanish football against Real's fiercest enemies. Barcelona stood in his way, determined to extend Madrid's long wait for the cup. Neither side backed down, but Real Madrid broke the deadlock. They defeated Barcelona 1–0 in Valencia. Mourinho secured his first Spanish silverware and ended Real Madrid's eighteen-year drought. It was also the club's first trophy since their 2007–08 La Liga title. The Special One delivered when it mattered most.
