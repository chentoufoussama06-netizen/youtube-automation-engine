Luis de la Fuente stared at the empty bench, knowing the final hung on a single breath. Could his fledgling squad survive the pressure of a first senior trophy? On 25 March 2023, 32-year-old debutant Joselu struck twice, delivering a 3-0 win over Norway and giving de Fuente his first senior victory. Weeks later, De Kuip in Rotterdam held its collective heartbeat as Spain and Croatia fought to a 0-0 deadlock. Unai Simón, the keeper he trusted from his youth ranks, saved Lovro Majer with his foot, then denied Bruno Petković, keeping the Spaniards alive. Dani Carvajal calmly chipped the decisive spot-kick, sealing a 5-4 penalty triumph and Spain’s inaugural Nations League crown. The stadium exhaled. Next came Euro 2024, and a 16-year-old winger.
