The Chipped Penalty That Won Spain a Trophy. On 25 March 2023, he unleashed his vision against Norway. 32-year-old debutant Joselu scored twice in a 3–0 victory. Then came Rotterdam on 18 June 2023. Spain faced Croatia in the Nations League final at De Kuip. The match ended in a tense 0–0 draw after extra time. It went to penalties. Simón saved twice, setting the stage. Dani Carvajal stepped up and chipped the winning kick into the net. Spain won 5–4, securing their first Nations League title. Next came Euro 2024, and a 16-year-old winger. Part three is next.
