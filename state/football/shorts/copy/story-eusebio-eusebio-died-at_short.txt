Seventy-one years. That is all Eusébio had before dying of heart failure. Eusébio died at his home of heart failure, aged 71. The Portuguese legend left behind an unparalleled legacy, but the reaction to his passing showed just how much he meant to the sport. The Portuguese government declared three days of national mourning. Hundreds of thousands came to pay tribute. On 9 January, his coffin was transported around the field of the Estádio da Luz. Days later, fans transformed his statue into a massive memorial, and every Benfica player wore his name on their shirts. The Black Panther was honored by the world he had transformed.
