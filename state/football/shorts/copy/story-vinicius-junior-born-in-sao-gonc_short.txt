Vinícius Júnior stepped onto Flamengo’s field at just sixteen. What would a 16-year-old debut mean for his future? He entered as an 82nd-minute substitute on 13 May 2017, watching Flamengo draw 1-1 with Atlético Mineiro. Two days later his contract rose to a €45 million buyout, and Real Madrid soon sealed a €46 million record deal for the teenager. Did that transfer reshape Brazilian football the global game forever?
