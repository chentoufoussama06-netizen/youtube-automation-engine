Roberto Baggio led Italy all the way to the 1994 World Cup final against Brazil, but drama awaited. Playing with a hamstring injury, Baggio battled through a scoreless match at the Rose Bowl. Everything hung on a nerve-wracking penalty shootout. Baggio stepped up to take Italy's last penalty. He put the decisive spot-kick over the crossbar, giving Brazil the World Cup title. Immediately, a shock of pain went through his leg. Baggio stood alone near the goal for eight minutes with his head down. He called it the worst moment of his career, a miss that haunted his dreams for years.
