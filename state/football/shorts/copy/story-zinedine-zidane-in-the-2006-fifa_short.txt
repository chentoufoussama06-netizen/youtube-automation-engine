Zinedine Zidane entered the 2006 World Cup final with a legend on the line. France faced Italy in Berlin, and seven minutes in Zidane struck a Panenka-style penalty that hit the crossbar and bounced over, making him only the fourth player to score in two different finals. He almost added a second, his header saved by Gianluigi Buffon. In extra-time, at the 110th minute, Zidane head-butted Marco Materazzi in the chest and was sent off, missing the shoot-out that Italy won 5-3. The Golden Ball followed, but his career ended in a flash of fury. A hero, forever flawed.
