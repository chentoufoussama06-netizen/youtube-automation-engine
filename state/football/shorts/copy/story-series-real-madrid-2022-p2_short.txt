Chelsea Had Them Beaten Then Rodrygo Came On. In the first leg of the Champions League quarter-finals, Real Madrid went away to Stamford Bridge. Benzema produced his second successive knockout hat-trick, driving Madrid to a 3–1 victory. Real Madrid suffered a 2–3 defeat on the night. The tie was slipping away, but substitute Rodrygo struck with a volley to send the game into extra time. In the 96th minute, Benzema rose to deliver a header into the net. Real Madrid won 5–4 on aggregate to reach the semi-finals, surviving another knife-edge tie. In the semi-final, Manchester City were ahead at the 90th minute. Part three is next.
