Gary Speed brought dramatic change to Welsh football, but his life ended in sudden, tragic shock. Speed started his professional playing career with Leeds United in 1988, winning the English First Division championship in 1991–92. He went on to captain Wales, becoming their most capped outfield player with 85 caps. In December 2010, Speed took charge as manager of the Wales national team. Under his leadership, Wales transformed. Following a 4–1 home win against Norway in November 2011, Wales were named FIFA’s 'Best Movers' of the year after climbing from 117th to 45th in the world. On 26 November 2011, Speed appeared on BBC One’s Football Focus, where presenter Dan Walker described him as being in fine form. That evening, he drove home to Huntington, Cheshire. The following morning, just before 7 a. on 27 November 2011, his wife Louise found him unresponsive in their garage. Police confirmed his death at age 42. Speed had taken his own life while still manager of Wales, leaving the football world in complete mourning. His vision laid the pathway for a legendary Welsh resurgence.
