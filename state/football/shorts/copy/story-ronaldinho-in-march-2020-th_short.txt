Ronaldinho's journey to a charity event ended with him arrested in Paraguay. Ronaldinho faced police questioning after entering Paraguay using a falsified passport. He traveled to the country for a charity event and book promotion. His lawyer could not explain why he used a fake passport, since Brazilian nationals do not need one to enter Mercosur countries. Held in custody, Ronaldinho competed in a prison futsal tournament. His team won the final 11–2, with Ronaldinho scoring five goals and assisting six. He attempted to appeal his detention order, but was ordered to house arrest until paying a plea deal fine to secure his release.
