Zinedine Zidane The Day He Said He’d Rather Die Than Apologise. Then, in the 110th minute, everything shattered. After Marco Materazzi insulted Zidane's sister, the French star headbutted the defender in the chest. Zidane was sent off, and France lost the penalty shootout 5–3. Years later, the headbutt remained an unforgettable open wound. In 2010, Zidane addressed the world-famous clash. He stated he would rather die than apologize to Materazzi for the strike. Yet he also admitted he could never have lived with himself if he had stayed on the pitch to help France win. Some regrets run deeper than victory.
