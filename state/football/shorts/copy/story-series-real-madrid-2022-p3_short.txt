Manchester City held a 5–3 aggregate lead at the 90th minute. Real Madrid were moments away from Champions League elimination, needing two goals just to survive. Then came Rodrygo. Just one minute later, between the 90th and 91st minutes, Rodrygo scored again to level the tie at 5–5 on aggregate. The Santiago Bernabéu erupted as the semi-final second leg was pushed into extra time. Manchester City were stunned, but Real Madrid were not finished. Karim Benzema stepped up and scored a decisive penalty in extra time, sealing a 3–1 victory on the night. Real Madrid progressed 6–5 on aggregate. Part four is next.
