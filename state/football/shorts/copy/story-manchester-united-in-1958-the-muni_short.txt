Manchester United faced their darkest hour in 1958 when tragedy struck the legendary Busby Babes. Manager Matt Busby had built a brilliant young team with an average age of just 22. They won back-to-back league titles in 1956 and 1957, and became the first English team to compete in the European Cup. The following season, the team traveled to play Red Star Belgrade. On 6 February 1958, on the way home from a European Cup quarter-final victory, their aircraft stopped to refuel in Munich, Germany. While attempting to take off, the plane crashed. The Munich air disaster claimed 23 lives. Eight United players were killed in the crash: Geoff Bent, Roger Byrne, Eddie Colman, Duncan Edwards, Mark Jones, David Pegg, Tommy Taylor, and Billy Whelan. Several others were injured, including Busby himself. Assistant manager Jimmy Murphy took over as manager while Busby recovered from his injuries. Murphy guided a makeshift side all the way to the FA Cup final, which they lost to Bolton Wanderers. Yet, Busby eventually returned to rebuild the club.
