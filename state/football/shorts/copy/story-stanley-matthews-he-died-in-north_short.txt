A sudden fall at a holiday home brought a devastating end. Sir Stanley Matthews suffered the accident in Tenerife. He passed away at the age of 85. On 23 February 2000, he died in the North Staffordshire Nuffield Hospital in Newcastle-under-Lyme. His death was announced on the radio just before an England versus Argentina friendly match started. More than 100,000 people lined the streets of Stoke-on-Trent to pay tribute. His ashes were finally buried beneath the centre circle of Stoke City's Stadium. The Magician was gone.
