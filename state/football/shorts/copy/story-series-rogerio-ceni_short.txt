129 goals. That is how many Rogério Ceni scored. Standing over a free kick for São Paulo, the shot-stopper prepared to attempt the unthinkable once again. As São Paulo’s official dead-ball specialist from 1997, he stepped up to take free kicks and penalties throughout his twenty-five-year career. On 27 March 2011, São Paulo faced their arch rivals, Corinthians. Ceni stepped up to take a free kick from 18 metres out and struck it into the net to win the match. It was his 100th goal, cementing him as the highest-scoring goalkeeper in football history.
