Bellingham found the net during a dramatic 6–4 victory. His strike brought his tournament total to seven goals, making his tally the outright most ever by an England player in a single World Cup edition. He helped England finish in third place and walked away with the Bronze Boot as the third-highest scorer of the tournament. Was this the greatest individual World Cup campaign by an English player?
