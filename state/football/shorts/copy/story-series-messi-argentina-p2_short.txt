Three finals lost in three years, and Lionel Messi walked away. Yet the pressure mounted as Argentina searched for elusive glory. He ended their twenty-eight-year trophy drought by defeating Brazil in the final of the twenty-twenty-one Copa América. Then, at the twenty-twenty-two FIFA World Cup, he scored twice in the final against France to secure Argentina's first World Cup in thirty-six years. Becoming the first player to win the Golden Ball twice, Messi claimed every top-tier trophy at club and country levels. It was a remarkable journey for the boy once considered too small to play.
