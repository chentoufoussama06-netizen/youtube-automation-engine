Diego Maradona faced a painful reality away from the pitch. Deep personal loss would eventually strike the Argentine football icon. Raised by a poor Roman Catholic family, he had four older sisters and two younger brothers. His parents had moved to Buenos Aires from Esquina in the 1950s. His father worked at a chemicals factory, while his mother was of Italian and Croatian descent. No matter how high his career soared, time inevitably caught up with his family. His mother, Dalma Salvadora Franco, died on 19 November 2011 at age 81. Then, his father, also named Diego Maradona, died on 25 June 2015 at age 87. The legendary playmaker lost both of his parents.
