Juventus stripped of titles and relegated in 2006. Juventus faced a massive investigation into alleged sporting fraud known as the Calciopoli scandal. The consequences were devastating. Juventus were stripped of their 2004–05 league title. Their 2005–06 title was later awarded to Inter Milan. Then came the ultimate punishment: Juventus were relegated to Serie B for the first time in their history. Several leading players left immediately after the relegation. But figures like Alessandro Del Piero, Gianluigi Buffon, Pavel Nedvěd, and David Trezeguet chose to remain. They won the 2006–07 Serie B title and immediately returned to Serie A.
