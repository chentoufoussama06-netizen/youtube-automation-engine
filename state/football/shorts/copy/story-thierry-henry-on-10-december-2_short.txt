Arsenal fans already declared Thierry Henry their greatest player ever. But could the club match that love for their all-time leading scorer? Henry scored 228 goals for Arsenal. He led them to two Premier League titles, three FA Cups, and an unbeaten Invincible season with 24 goals and 20 assists the year before. He even broke Ian Wright’s record of 185 goals to become their top scorer of all time. On 10 December 2011, Arsenal delivered the ultimate honor at the Emirates Stadium. As part of its 125th anniversary celebrations, the club unveiled a bronze statue of Henry. The man who gave everything to Arsenal was permanently frozen in bronze outside their home.
