Luis de la Fuente led Spain to the 2026 World Cup final. Standing in their way were defending champions Argentina at MetLife Stadium. Argentina went down to ten players after Enzo Fernández was sent off in the second half. The match remained scoreless through regulation time. In the second period of extra time, Nico Williams headed the ball to Ferran Torres. Torres struck it with his left foot from close range past Emiliano Martínez to make it 1–0. Luis de la Fuente became the first manager to win the Nations League, the European Championship, and the World Cup, and the oldest to win the World Cup.
