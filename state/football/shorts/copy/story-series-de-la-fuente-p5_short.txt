The Youth Coach Who Won Everything. From the first whistle, Spain dominated possession, carving chance after chance while Argentina’s Emiliano Martínez kept them at bay. In the second half Argentina were reduced to ten men when Enzo Fernández saw red, yet they still failed to register a single shot in regulation time—a World Cup final first. Extra time arrived, and in the 102nd minute Mikel Merino headed the ball wide. Moments later, Ferran Torres met a Nico Williams header, struck it cleanly, and sealed a 1–0 victory. De la Fuente became the first manager to win the Nations League, the European Championship and the World Cup, and the oldest ever World Cup-winning coach.
