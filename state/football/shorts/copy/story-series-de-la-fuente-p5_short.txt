Luis de la Fuente watched the clock tick down in MetLife Stadium, tension crackling. The match stayed deadlocked 0–0 through 90 minutes, Argentina’s Emiliano Martínez making eleven saves, a World Cup final record. In the 102nd minute Merino headed wide, then in the first period of extra time Ferran Torres received Nico Williams’ header and struck past Martínez for a 1–0 lead. Argentina pressed, missed chances, and a post-match brawl erupted. Spain lifted the trophy, and de la Fuente became the first manager to win the Nations League, the European Championship and the World Cup, and the oldest ever to win the World Cup.
