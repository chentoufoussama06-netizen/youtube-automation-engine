Thirty-nine football fans were killed before a single ball was even kicked. The decrepit Heysel Stadium was about to host the 1985 European Cup final between Juventus and Liverpool. An hour before kick-off, aggression flared between rival supporters across a flimsy divide. Objects were thrown back and forth, and the chicken wire fence was ripped down. Fans in the neutral section fled from involved Liverpool supporters toward the pitch, the exits, and a decrepit wall. Trapped against the structure, people were crushed until the wall collapsed under the force. Thirty-nine spectators died, and 600 more were injured. English clubs were subsequently banned from worldwide football matches.
