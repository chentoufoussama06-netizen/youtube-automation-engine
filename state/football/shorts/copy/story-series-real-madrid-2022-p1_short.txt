Karim Benzema orchestrated a breathtaking Champions League comeback for Real Madrid against Paris Saint-Germain. On 9 March 2022, Madrid faced PSG at the Bernabéu in the second leg of the round of 16. The stakes were absolute as Madrid fought to progress to the quarter-finals. Suddenly, Benzema flipped the entire match on its head. Within a stunning 17 minutes, the striker scored a hat-trick to power Real to a 3–1 victory on the night and a 3–2 win on aggregate. At 34, Benzema became the oldest player to score a Champions League hat-trick, bringing his tally to 309 goals and overtaking Alfredo Di Stéfano as Real Madrid's third-highest all-time goalscorer.
