Six months. That is how long Kolo Touré was suspended from football. He had just scored the first goal in a 4–3 win against Wolves that sent his team to the top of the Premier League table. Then, everything changed. On 3 March 2011, it was revealed that Touré had failed a drug test and had been suspended. The World Anti-Doping Agency imposed a 6-month suspension from football effective 2 March 2011. He returned the following season, making 14 league appearances as Manchester City won a league title for the first time in 44 years.
