Coroner's ambiguous verdict on Speed's death. An official inquest into his death convened, aiming to uncover the truth behind the tragedy. The hearing revealed that management pressure had strained his marriage, leading to an argument with his wife, Louise, the night before he died. Yet Louise testified that recent conversations focused on their exciting future together, while Alan Shearer had reminded Speed that such arguments were normal. Ultimately, the coroner reached a narrative verdict, ruling there was insufficient evidence to prove intent. Speed may have simply intended to make a dramatic gesture.
