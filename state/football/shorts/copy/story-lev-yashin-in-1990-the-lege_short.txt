Lev Yashin passed away at age 60. The football world suffered a shocking loss. Years earlier, he contracted thrombophlebitis in Budapest. That illness required the amputation of one of his legs. Despite surgical intervention to save his life, he died of stomach cancer in 1990. He left behind a legacy as the greatest goalkeeper in the history of the sport, the only one to ever win the Ballon d'Or. He received a state funeral as a Soviet Honoured Master of Sport. The man known as the Black Spider was gone.
