Roberto Baggio was one of the greatest players of all time, but one moment haunted him forever. At the 1994 World Cup, Baggio single-handedly hauled Italy to the final. He was the star performer of the tournament. But in the decisive shootout against Brazil, everything collapsed. Franco Baresi and Daniele Massaro had already missed their penalties. Then, Baggio stepped up. He missed the decisive penalty. Immediately after, Baggio felt a shock of pain go through his leg. He stood near the goal alone for eight minutes with his head down, earning the moniker "The Man who Died Standing." He finished runner-up for the Ballon d'Or, but the miss became the worst moment of his career. It affected him for years, and he still dreams about it to this day. Four years later, at the 1998 World Cup against Chile, Italy received a late penalty. Despite his trauma, Baggio stepped up to take it. He scored the equalizing goal, describing the moment as liberating. He became the first Italian to score in three World Cups, but the pain of 1994 never truly faded. The lasting memory of his career remains that single missed penalty.
