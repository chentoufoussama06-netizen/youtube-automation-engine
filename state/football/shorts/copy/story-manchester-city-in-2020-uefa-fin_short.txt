Thirty million euros. That is what UEFA handed Manchester City in February 2020. UEFA found the club guilty of serious breaches of the UEFA Financial Fair Play regulations by overstating its sponsorship revenue in its accounts and in the break-even information submitted to UEFA between 2012 and 2016. Alongside the fine, the club faced a ban on UEFA Champions League participation for two years. However, Manchester City appealed to the Court of Arbitration for Sport. The court overturned the Champions League ban and reduced the fine to ten million euros, stating that most of the alleged breaches were either not established or time-barred.
