Seventy-eight minutes. That was how long Fabrice Muamba had no heartbeat. During a 2012 FA Cup quarter-final between Bolton Wanderers and Tottenham Hotspur, the central midfielder collapsed on the pitch after suffering a cardiac arrest. Referee Howard Webb abandoned the match while medical personnel rushed to save him. Andrew Deaner, a cardiologist attending as a fan, joined the rescue effort. Muamba received numerous defibrillator shocks on the grass and inside the ambulance, but his heart refused to beat for over an hour. Against all odds, he recovered, eventually retiring from professional football on medical advice. Was this football's ultimate miracle?
