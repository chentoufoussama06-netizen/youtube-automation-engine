Eric Cantona’s infamous “kung-fu” kick stunned the world. What would happen when a star striker, already fighting for the title, met a screaming fan? In January 1995 Manchester United were chasing a third straight league crown. Cantona had already netted the winner against Blackburn on 22 January and added a goal in the FA Cup third round at Sheffield United. On 25 January, at Selhurst Park, he was sent off for kicking Palace defender Richard Shaw. As he walked toward the tunnel, Crystal Palace supporter Matthew Simmons rushed down eleven rows of stairs, shouting abuse. Cantona launched a flying kick into the crowd, then threw punches. A moment that still divides opinion.
