Harry Kane made football history in 2026. The English striker took the pitch for Bayern Munich, hunting for major trophies and ultimate glory. During a stunning 2025–26 season, Kane dominated the opposition. He scored 14 goals in the Champions League and totalled 61 goals for his club, helping Bayern Munich win the double. On 26 October 2026, the incredible payoff arrived at the London Palladium. Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or and his second Gerd Müller Trophy. He became the first Englishman to win the prestigious award since Michael Owen in 2001, and the first Bayern player to claim it since 1981.
