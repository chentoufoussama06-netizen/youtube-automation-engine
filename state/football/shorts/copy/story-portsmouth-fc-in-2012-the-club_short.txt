Portsmouth FC Plummets Into Administration. Nicknamed Pompey, this historic English team was once riding high. They won the FA Cup in 2008, but their seven-year stay in the Premier League ended with relegation. This signaled the beginning of a highly difficult period for the club. On 17 February 2012, Portsmouth went into financial administration for the second time in just two years. The consequences were swift and devastating. The club was hit with an automatic 10-point deduction. They were relegated from the Championship in 2011–12. It was the first time in 30 years that the club had played in the third tier of English football. Following this painful relegation to League One, the entire professional playing squad left the club. It was a staggering fall from grace for a famous team. A proud club was left completely depleted, facing an uncertain future in the lower leagues. Pompey had hit rock bottom.
