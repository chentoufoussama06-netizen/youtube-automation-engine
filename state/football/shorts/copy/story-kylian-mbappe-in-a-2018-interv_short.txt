By age nineteen, Kylian Mbappé had already won the World Cup with France. But behind his rapid rise to football stardom was a massive personal sacrifice. In a 2018 interview with Time, Mbappé opened up about the heavy cost of his commitment to football development. He explained that he did not have the moments of so-called normal people during adolescence, like going out with friends or enjoying good times. Instead of a typical youth, his life was completely turned upside down after he became famous. Just over four years after making his professional debut, he had gained over 50 million Instagram followers. Mbappé traded the ordinary experiences of growing up to reach the summit of world football.
