An effigy of Vinícius Júnior was hanged from a bridge in Madrid. Fans in Spain subjected the Real Madrid forward to racist slurs and chants. Four men were arrested by the Spanish Police in connection with the effigy. Vinícius condemned La Liga for normalising racism. Real Madrid reported the incident as a hate crime to the Attorney General's Office. FIFA president Gianni Infantino selected Vinícius to lead a special FIFA anti-racism committee. Support poured in from around the world. Brazilian president Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva condemned the racism. The Christ the Redeemer statue in Rio de Janeiro switched off its lights. Anti-racism legislation called Vini Jr. laws were approved in Rio de Janeiro and Rio Grande do Sul.
