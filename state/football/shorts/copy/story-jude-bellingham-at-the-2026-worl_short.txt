Jude Bellingham stood on the brink of World immortality. England needed a hero for place. He scored in the 4–2 win over Croatia, added a crucial goal and an assist versus Panama. In the round of 16 he netted two versus Mexico, then a brace against Norway. Bellingham scored again, finishing with seven goals—the most by an England player in a World Cup. Did he change England’s legacy?
