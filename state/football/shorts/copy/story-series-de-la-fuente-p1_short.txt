Luis de la Fuente stared at the empty bench, the clock ticking down. On 8 December 2022 the federation named the youth-team maestro as senior coach, handing him a contract that ran to Euro 2024 with an option to extend. He had guided the under-19s to the 2015 European title, the under-21s to the 2019 championship, and earned an Olympic silver in Tokyo. Four days later he was officially presented, his résumé a string of youth trophies, his philosophy a simple conviction: trust the players you’ve raised. The pressure was immense, the expectations sky-high. All eyes waited for his first move. His first final was in Rotterdam, against Croatia. Part two is next.
