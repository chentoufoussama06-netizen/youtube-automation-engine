How did Scarborough’s 2007 liquidation spark a fan‑owned phoenix club? Scarborough Football Club, one of England’s oldest sides, was wound up on 20 June 2007 after accumulating debts of £2. 5 million, ending a continuous presence at the Athletic Ground since 1898. The liquidation left the town’s Seadogs without a team and the historic McCain Stadium empty, a stark reminder of the club’s abrupt demise. Within weeks, the supporters who had watched generations of matches gathered under the banner of the Seadog Trust, determined to preserve their footballing identity. On 25 June 2007 they founded Scarborough Athletic, a supporter‑owned phoenix club built from the ashes of the original organization.
