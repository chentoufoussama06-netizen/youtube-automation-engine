Neymar’s 2019 rape accusation rocks his career. In May 2019, Neymar joined Brazil’s 23-man squad for the Copa América. But trouble mounted off the field, and a rape investigation was launched against the Brazilian star. Tension peaked as the story swept through the football world. Then, in July 2019, police announced the rape investigation against Neymar would be dropped due to insufficient evidence. He was cleared, but the controversy contributed to a black year off the field, keeping him off the 30-man Ballon d'Or shortlist for the first time since 2010. A nightmare cleared, but the damage was done.
