Twelve months. That is how long André Onana was banned by UEFA. That was the punishment handed down to André Onana. In February 2021, UEFA banned the goalkeeper after he tested positive for furosemide, a banned substance. Onana stated he took his wife's medicine by mistake. Ajax joined him in appealing against the decision. Later in June, the Court of Arbitration for Sport reduced the ban to nine months. He ultimately spent seven and a half years with Ajax, making 214 appearances in all competitions during his stint before leaving the club.
