Zlatan Ibrahimović had thirty-four trophies, but his face was banned. His wife simply did not allow it. The Swedish former professional footballer scored over 570 career goals, won 34 trophies, and earned multiple individual accolades in the United States after joining LA Galaxy in March 2018. He was frequently mentioned in media for his personality and physical dominance. Yet inside his own house, his famous face was completely banned from the walls. In 2018, Ibrahimović revealed the one exception to her strict rule. His wife permitted just a single photograph to hang inside their home. "There is one of my feet on the wall," he explained. That single image served as a reminder of what built their life.
