Kylian Mbappé sparked an international outrage after laughing at a viral meme on live television. On 26 October 2026, the Real Madrid forward appeared on the French talk show The Bridge. During the broadcast, Mbappé stated that a meme of a Chinese dictator made him laugh the most, referring to Mao Zedong. As he spoke, Mao’s portrait appeared superimposed with Mbappé's face. The backlash was instant. Chinese brands like Eastroc Beverage, Mengniu Dairy, and Milkground immediately terminated their deals with him. China Central Television even suspended La Liga matches.
