Diego Maradona faced his final battle in November 2020, ending the life of a football legend. As head coach of Gimnasia de La Plata, Maradona suffered from health issues. On 2 November 2020, he entered a hospital in La Plata for anemia, dehydration, and depression. The next day, he underwent emergency brain surgery for a subdural hematoma. Sedated later for alcohol withdrawal symptoms, doctors released him on 12 November for outpatient care. Then came the tragic blow. On 25 November 2020, Maradona suffered cardiac arrest and died in his sleep at home in Dique Luján, Buenos Aires Province. The world lost a true icon.
