A Munich air disaster survivor, Bobby Charlton has died at 86. In the 1966 World Cup semi-final against Portugal, Charlton scored twice, including a shot following a run and pull-back from Geoff Hurst, sending England to the final. That same year, Charlton won the FIFA World Cup and the Ballon d'Or. He survived the 1958 Munich air disaster, captained Manchester United to European Cup victory in 1968, and scored 49 goals in 106 caps for England. Charlton died on 21 October 2023 at age 86. His death leaves Geoff Hurst as the last surviving English player of the 1966 World Cup final.
