Norway were twenty-five years without a win against Italy. Norway's World Cup qualifying match hung in the balance, and Erling Haaland needed to break the quarter-century curse. The Norwegian striker had already proven his deadly finishing talent across Europe. Now, facing Italy on 6 June 2025, Norway fought to turn their fortunes around in a crucial qualifier. Haaland stepped up and scored Norway's final goal of the match, wrapping up a 3–0 World Cup qualifying victory. That single goal sealed Norway's first win over Italy in 25 years. Erling Haaland delivered the long-awaited triumph his country had spent a quarter of a century waiting for.
