A fatal crowd crush unfolded during the Hillsborough disaster. For decades, grieving families demanded truth about the police actions. The second coroner's inquests ran from 1 April 2014 to 26 April 2016. The jury returned a verdict of unlawful killing in respect of all 96 victims. They ruled that the supporters were unlawfully killed owing to grossly negligent failures by police and ambulance services. The inquests found that the design of the stadium contributed to the crush, and that supporters were not to blame. The long fight for justice finally brought accountability.
