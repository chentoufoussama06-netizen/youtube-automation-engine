Erling Haaland walked onto Salzburg’s field, the pressure palpable. Could a teenager from Bryne and Molde really dominate Europe’s biggest stage? He signed for Red Bull Salzburg in 2019 and immediately helped them capture the Austrian Bundesliga and Cup. His Champions League heroics, scoring in the first five group matches, caught the eye of Borussia Dortmund. Dortmund signed him in December 2019, launching the career of a future world-class striker. Did that bold move change football forever?
