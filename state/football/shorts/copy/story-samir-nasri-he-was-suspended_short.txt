Eighteen months. That is how long Samir Nasri was suspended from football. Samir Nasri was about to watch his career slip away. In February 2018, UEFA hit the French midfielder with a six-month ban for breaching WADA rules. Back in December 2016, he had received an intravenous drip containing 500 millilitres of water and nutrients. Then came the appeal from UEFA's ethics and disciplinary inspector. On 1 August 2018, his suspension was increased by an extra twelve months, back-dated to 1 July 2017. Nasri was suspended from football for eighteen months until January 2019 following the doping violation.
