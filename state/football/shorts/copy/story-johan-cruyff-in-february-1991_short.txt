Eighty. That is how many cigarettes a day Johan Cruyff smoked. At times, he smoked eighty cigarettes a day, continuing the vice deep into his professional career. Then, the dangerous habit caught up with him. In February 1991, Cruyff suffered a heart attack. He survived and had to undergo bypass surgery. The scare changed everything for the football icon. Cruyff quit smoking immediately and became the face of anti-smoking campaigns, famously kicking away a cigarette pack in a Catalan health department advertisement.
