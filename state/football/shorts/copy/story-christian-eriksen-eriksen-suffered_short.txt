Christian Eriksen collapsed on the pitch after suffering a cardiac arrest. During UEFA Euro 2020 on 12 June 2021, midfielder Christian Eriksen collapsed on the pitch in the 42nd minute against Finland. He was about to receive a throw-in when he went down. Eriksen had suffered a cardiac arrest. Captain Simon Kjær placed him into the recovery position as medical help arrived immediately. CPR and defibrillation were performed right on the field before he was carried off on a stretcher. Eriksen was stabilized at Rigshospitalet Hospital. He was later fitted with an ICD device to regulate his heart.
