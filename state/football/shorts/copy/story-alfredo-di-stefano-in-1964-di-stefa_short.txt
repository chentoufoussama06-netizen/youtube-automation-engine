Three days after being kidnapped, Alfredo Di Stéfano lost a cup final. While in Venezuela for an exhibition tour, Alfredo Di Stéfano became the target of an armed group. On 24 August 1963, the National Liberation Armed Forces of Venezuela kidnapped the Argentine champion from the Potomac hotel in Caracas. For three days, Madrid held their breath. Without Di Stéfano, Real Madrid lost the Small Club World Cup final game to São Paulo. He survived the kidnapping, but Los Blancos lost the trophy.
