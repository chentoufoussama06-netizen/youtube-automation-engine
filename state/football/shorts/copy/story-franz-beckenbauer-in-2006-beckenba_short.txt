Franz Beckenbauer led Germany's successful bid to host the 2006 FIFA World Cup and chaired the organizing committee. But his reputation as an administrator was soon tarnished by repeated allegations of bribery. Beginning in August 2016, Beckenbauer was investigated for fraud and money laundering in connection with the 2006 World Cup. The investigation was closed without a verdict in 2020 as the period for bringing a charge under the statute of limitations had expired. The dark shadow remained.
