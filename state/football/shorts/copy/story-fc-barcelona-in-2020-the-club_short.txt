FC Barcelona faced an unthinkable crisis when severe financial troubles forced them to part ways with Lionel Messi. Messi was the club's all-time leading goalscorer with 672 goals. He had made 778 official appearances for the team. But worsening finances and decline on the pitch created rising dissatisfaction among supporters. Josep Maria Bartomeu resigned as president on October 27, 2020, to avoid a vote of no confidence. Joan Laporta became president in March 2021, but massive financial problems remained. Barcelona found themselves unable to comply with La Liga's Financial Fair Play requirements. Negotiations with Messi had been ongoing, and both sides actually reached an agreement over the details of a new contract. However, on August 5, 2021, Barcelona announced that La Liga regulations made it impossible to re-sign him. After 21 years as a Barça player, Messi departed the club and signed on a free transfer with French club Paris Saint-Germain. A legendary era shattered in an instant.
