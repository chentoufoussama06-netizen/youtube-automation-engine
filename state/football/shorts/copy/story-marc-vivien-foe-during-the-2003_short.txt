Marc-Vivien Foé collapsed on the pitch in 2003, sparking a tragedy that shocked world football forever. It was the Confederations Cup semi-final between Cameroon and Colombia at the Stade de Gerland in Lyon. Cameroon led 1–0 through a goal by Pius N'Diefi. In the 72nd minute, defensive midfielder Foé suddenly collapsed in the centre circle with no players near him. Medics rushed to perform mouth-to-mouth resuscitation and spent 45 minutes trying to restart his heart. Tragically, he died shortly afterwards from hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, a heart condition causing sudden death during physical exercise. A hero was lost, but his memory lives on forever.
