Three senior members of George Weah’s cabinet faced American sanctions for corruption. The president promised to fight graft, yet his administration faced mounting pressure as international scrutiny intensified. Weah, the former footballer who became Liberia's 25th president in 2018, initially cited economic reform and improved living conditions as his main targets. However, on 15 August 2022, the United States sanctioned three of his cabinet members for alleged corruption. This move followed criticism regarding his governance and unfulfilled pledges, including delays in establishing specialized courts and forensic equipment for sexual violence cases. The promise of change met a harsh reality.
