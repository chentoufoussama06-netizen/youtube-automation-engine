Eight months. That is how long Rio Ferdinand was banned from football. In September 2003, he left United's Carrington training ground for shopping, attempting to return too late. The Football Association Disciplinary Committee imposed an eight-month ban starting in January 2004 alongside a £50,000 fine. United appealed the verdict, but the original decision stood. The ban forced Ferdinand to miss half a Premier League season, Manchester United's FA Cup triumph, and the Euro 2004 international competition. He finally made his return in September.
