Real Madrid entered a 36-minute delayed kickoff with the title on the line. Could a single breakthrough survive Liverpool’s relentless barrage? Alexander-Arnold’s early crosses found Salah and Mané, both denied by Courtois. In the 59th minute Valverde delivered a low cross; Vinícius tucked it past Alisson for 1-0. Liverpool pressed, Salah shot twice, Jota and Firmino tried, yet Courtois saved every attempt. The final whistle sealed a 1-0 victory, Real’s 14th crown. Was this the climax of the impossible run?
