Der Bomber scored goals at will. Yet, the ultimate goal poacher faced a final battle he could not win. Gerd Müller joined Bayern Munich in 1963 and scored 365 goals in 427 Bundesliga matches, won the FIFA World Cup, the UEFA Champions League, and the Ballon d'Or, and fired 68 goals in 62 appearances for West Germany. Then, health problems mounted. On 6 October 2015, it was announced that Müller was suffering from Alzheimer's disease. He died on 15 August 2021 in a nursing home in Wolfratshausen, Bavaria, aged 75. Der Bomber is gone.
