Thirty years old. That is when Marco van Basten’s career ended. The Dutch striker had scored over 300 goals and won the Ballon d'Or, but a recurring ankle injury threatened everything. During the European Cup final against Marseille, a hard tackle from behind by Basile Boli forced Van Basten off in the eighty-sixth minute. It condemned him to his third ankle surgery. He spent two whole years on the sidelines undergoing unsuccessful treatment. Milan even ordered him to miss the 1994 World Cup to protect his rehabilitation. On 17 August 1995, Van Basten finally announced his retirement. Pain forced one of football's greatest talents off the pitch forever.
