Gary Speed was the manager driving the Welsh national team to stunning new heights before his life suddenly ended. In 2011, he transformed Wales from their lowest ever FIFA ranking of 117th up to 45th in the world. On 26 November 2011, Speed appeared as a guest on Football Focus, looking in fine form. He watched a match with Alan Shearer, texted a friend, and drove home to Huntington. The following morning, his wife Louise found him unresponsive in their garage. He had hanged himself. Police confirmed the 42-year-old’s death. It was reported as a suicide, leaving a legacy that inspired Welsh football for decades.
