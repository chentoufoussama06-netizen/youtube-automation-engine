John Terry faced a career-altering crisis when accused of racially abusing Anton Ferdinand during a heated match. Terry claimed he was merely repeating what he thought Ferdinand said to him. The scandal escalated rapidly. The FA stripped Terry of his England captaincy, prompting manager Capello to resign. Ferdinand then refused to shake Terry's hand. In July 2012, a criminal court acquitted Terry of the charge. But the FA held its own hearing. Terry retired from international football, and the FA found him guilty. Terry received a four-match ban and a massive two-hundred-and-twenty-thousand-pound fine.
