Mohamed Salah shattered the Premier League record with 32 goals. Could that fire still push Liverpool to a Champions League final? In his debut season he set the 38-game goal record. He also helped Liverpool reach the 2018 final. The historic run delivered Liverpool’s first final in eleven years and crowned Salah the Egyptian King. What moment defined his breakthrough?
