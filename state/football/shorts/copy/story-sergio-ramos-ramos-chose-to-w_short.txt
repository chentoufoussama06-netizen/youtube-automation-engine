Ninety-three minutes. That is how long Sergio Ramos waited to make history. What drove the defender to abandon the traditional number four he wore for years? On 6 February 2025, Ramos was announced as a new player for Liga MX side Monterrey. Looking ahead to a fresh chapter, he faced a massive decision regarding his jersey. Instead of his classic number, Ramos chose to wear number 93. This choice carried immense meaning from his past. He selected it specifically to honor his 93rd-minute equaliser in the 2014 UEFA Champions League final for Real Madrid. That crucial header had tied the game 1-1 against Atlético Madrid before Real won 4-1 in extra-time to claim La Decima, their tenth trophy in the competition. Now, that exact minute lives forever on his back in Mexico.
