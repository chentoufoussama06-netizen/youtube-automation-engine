Leicester City produced a thrilling English football story during a wild turn of events. In their 2014–15 season, the Foxes spent four-and-a-half months marooned at the bottom of the Premier League table. By 3 April 2015, they sat seven points adrift from safety with just 19 points from 29 matches. No team with fewer than 20 points at that stage had ever stayed up. Instead, Leicester mounted one of the greatest escapes in Premier League history, winning seven of their last nine fixtures to finish 14th on 41 points. But turmoil hit on 30 June 2015 when manager Nigel Pearson was dismissed. On 13 July 2015, Claudio Ranieri was appointed as the new manager to an initially sceptical reaction, and bookmakers set Leicester as 5000–1 underdogs to win the division. Then a striker who came from non-League football started scoring, and he would not stop. Part two is next.
