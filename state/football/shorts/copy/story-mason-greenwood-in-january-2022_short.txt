Mason Greenwood was arrested by Greater Manchester Police. On 30 January 2022, United suspended Greenwood after social media posts accused him of assault. Police initially arrested him on suspicion of rape and assault, later arresting him on suspicion of sexual assault and threats to kill. Nike stopped sponsoring him, and EA Sports removed him from FIFA games. Although the Crown Prosecution Service eventually dropped all charges in February 2023, United conducted their own investigation. They ultimately announced Greenwood would leave the club.
