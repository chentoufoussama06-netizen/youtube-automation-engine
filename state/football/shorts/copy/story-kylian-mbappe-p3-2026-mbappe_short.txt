Kylian Mbappé’s saga continues, this time with a courtroom twist. On 26 October 2026, during the talk-show The Bridge, Mbappé joked about a meme featuring a Chinese leader, prompting Chinese brands Eastroc Beverage, Mengniu Dairy and Milkground to cut ties and CCTV to suspend La Liga matches. The foreign ministry warned of “opposition” to any hurtful words. Mbappé issued a sincere apology the next day, and Real Madrid and La Liga issued distancing statements. The fallout reminded everyone that fame can turn fragile in an instant.
