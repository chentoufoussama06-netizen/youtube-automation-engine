Mikel Merino's 119th-minute header sent Germany home. France struck first, Randal Kolo Muani heading in the 9th minute. Yamal answered in the 21st, curling a shot from outside the box to level. Dani Olmo then netted the winner in the 25th, turning the tie 2–1. Both Merino and Olmo had lifted the 2019 under-21 trophy under de Fuente, now repeating history. Spain marched to the final, their youth-grown heroes proving de Fuente’s gamble. The stadium held its breath, tight. Could a teenager really carry a nation to glory? Part four is next.
