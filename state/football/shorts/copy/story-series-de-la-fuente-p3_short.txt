Lamine Yamal equalised, but France still led. Spain needed a single strike to stay alive. Randal Kolo Muani opened the scoring for France with a header in the ninth minute. Then, in the 21st minute, Yamal unleashed a curling shot from outside the penalty area, finding the left corner. Four minutes later, Dani Olmo tucked away the winner, turning a 1-0 deficit into a 2-1 triumph. The goal made Yamal the youngest scorer in European Championship history and sent Spain to their first final since 2012. The stadium held its breath, still utterly stunned. One game left: England, in Berlin. Part four is next.
