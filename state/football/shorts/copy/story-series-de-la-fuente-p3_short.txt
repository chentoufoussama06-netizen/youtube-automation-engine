A 16-year-old winger was about to change European football history. Spain entered Euro 2024 with momentum, sweeping their group with five goals scored and zero conceded. But the knockout rounds brought chaos. In the quarter-final against hosts Germany, Dani Olmo opened the scoring before a late equaliser forced extra time. Then, in the 119th minute, Mikel Merino headed in the winner to shatter German hopes. In the semi-final, France took an early lead through Randal Kolo Muani. Spain needed a miracle, and Lamine Yamal delivered. The teenager curled a shot from outside the penalty area into the left corner, becoming the youngest goalscorer in Euro history before Olmo scored the winner. Part four is next.
