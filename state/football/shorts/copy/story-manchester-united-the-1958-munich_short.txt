Manchester United faced heartbreak in 1958 after making history in European football. Matt Busby built a young squad with an average age of 22, nicknamed the Busby Babes. They won back-to-back league titles in 1956 and 1957, and became the first English team to compete in the European Cup. The following season, the team secured a European Cup quarter-final victory against Red Star Belgrade. But on the way home, disaster struck. The aircraft carrying the players, officials, and journalists crashed while attempting to take off after refuelling in Munich, Germany. The Munich air disaster of 6 February 1958 claimed 23 lives, including eight Manchester United players. It permanently altered the club's history.
