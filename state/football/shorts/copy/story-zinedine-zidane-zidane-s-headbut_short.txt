When Zidane Lost His Cool on the World Stage. The world of football already knew this second World Cup final was to be the last match of his career. Zidane had already announced he was to retire after the expiration of his Real Madrid contract at the end of the 2005–06 season. The stakes in Berlin were incredibly high. Seven minutes into the final against Italy, Zidane put France ahead with a Panenka-style penalty kick which struck the crossbar and bounced just over the goal line. This made him only the fourth player in World Cup history to score in two different finals. He almost scored a second goal during the first period of extra time, but his header was saved by Italy's goalkeeper Gianluigi Buffon. Then, the match took a dark turn. In the 110th minute of the game, Zidane was sent off after headbutting Marco Materazzi in the chest. It was later revealed through interviews that Materazzi had insulted Zidane's sister, triggering his anger. Without their captain, France had to play on. Zidane did not participate in the penalty shootout, which Italy won 5–3. This marked the 14th overall expulsion of Zidane's career. He became the fourth player red-carded in a World Cup final, and the first ever sent off in extra time. The headbutt made headlines all over the world. In France, Le Figaro called his action "odious." Yet, the day after the final, Zidane was still awarded the Golden Ball as the player of the tournament. Upon his return to France, thousands of fans filled the Place de la Concorde in Paris, chanting "Zizou! Zizou!" Years later, Zidane stated that he would rather die than apologize to Materazzi for the headbutt. However, he also admitted that he could never have lived with himself had he been allowed to remain on the pitch and help France win. A genius defined by both magic and madness.
