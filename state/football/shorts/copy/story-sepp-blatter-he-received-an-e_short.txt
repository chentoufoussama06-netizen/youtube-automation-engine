Eight years. That was the ban handed to FIFA president Sepp Blatter. He had just won a fifth term in May 2015, but a massive corruption scandal was exploding around him. Criminal proceedings were opened by the Swiss Attorney General's office for criminal mismanagement and misappropriation. In October, Blatter was suspended. Then, in December 2015, the independent FIFA Ethics Committee ejected him from office and banned him from all FIFA activities for eight years. A FIFA appeals committee later reduced that ban to six years. But in 2021, a probe into massive bonus payments brought a second six-year ban. The man who ruled world football was out.
