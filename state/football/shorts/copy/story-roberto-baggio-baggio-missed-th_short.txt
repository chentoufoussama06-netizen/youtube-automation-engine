Roberto Baggio dragged Italy to the 1994 World Cup final, only to face ultimate heartbreak. Playing through a hamstring injury at the Pasadena Rose Bowl, Baggio battled against Brazil. After extra time ended 0–0, the world title came down to a penalty shootout. Franco Baresi and Daniele Massaro had already missed for Italy. With the championship on the line, Baggio stepped up to take the decisive spot-kick. He put it over the crossbar. Brazil won the World Cup. Baggio stood alone by the goal for eight minutes, head down. It became the worst moment of his career, haunting his dreams for decades.
