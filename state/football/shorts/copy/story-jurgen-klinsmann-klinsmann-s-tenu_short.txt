Dead last. That is where Jürgen Klinsmann left the United States. That was the disaster that left the United States at the bottom of the qualification table. through its first round of World Cup qualification out of a group containing Guatemala, Trinidad and Tobago, and St. Vincent and the Grenadines. But the final World Cup qualification round opened in November 2016 with a 2–1 home defeat to Mexico, followed by the heavy loss in Costa Rica. Following these losses, the USSF fired Klinsmann on 21 November 2016. He was replaced by Bruce Arena.
