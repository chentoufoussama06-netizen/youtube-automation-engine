David Beckham took the pitch wearing boots stitched with his family's names. He had already announced his retirement, but his final home game held one last moment of tension. Playing for Paris Saint-Germain against Brest, Beckham was made captain. He delivered a corner kick that assisted a goal by Matuidi, marking the final play of his professional career. In the 79th minute, the manager subbed him off for Ezequiel Lavezzi. Teammates hugged him, and the crowd gave him a standing ovation as he tearfully waved goodbye to the fans. PSG went on to win the match 3–1. A 21-year playing career ended with tears, a victory, and one final assist.
