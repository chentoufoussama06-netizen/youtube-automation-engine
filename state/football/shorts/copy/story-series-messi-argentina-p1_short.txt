After losing three consecutive finals, Lionel Messi retired from international football. After Argentina built their team around their captain, the ultimate prize kept slipping away. In 2014, Messi won the Golden Ball, but Germany beat Argentina in the World Cup final. The heartbreak deepened in 2015 when Argentina lost the Copa América final to Chile. One year later, history repeated itself as Chile defeated Argentina again in the 2016 Copa América Centenario final. Crushed by the repeated agony, Messi announced his retirement from international football. Desperate to change his mind, fans built a bronze statue of him in Buenos Aires. A nationwide campaign brought him back. Part two is next.
