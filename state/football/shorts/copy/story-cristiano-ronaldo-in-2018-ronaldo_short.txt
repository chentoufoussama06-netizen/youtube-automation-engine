Cristiano Ronaldo faced massive legal drama in Spain when tax charges hit the football superstar. In 2017, authorities charged CR7 with fraudulently evading almost 15 million euros in tax between 2011 and 2014. At the time, the Portuguese forward denied the claim. Serious consequences hung over him, but he reached a deal with Spanish authorities in 2018. Ronaldo received a two-year suspended prison sentence for tax evasion. He served the sentence under probation without any prison time, on the condition that he would not re-offend. Even off the pitch, his actions carried huge consequences.
