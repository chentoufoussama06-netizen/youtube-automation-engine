Neymar faced off-field controversies and an intense police investigation that made 2019 a true black year for him. The Brazilian superstar was already navigating a chaotic season. Injuries disrupted his playing time for Paris Saint-Germain, including a right foot metatarsal reinjury in January. He insulted video review officials on social media after PSG lost to Manchester United, resulting in a three-match UEFA ban. After the Coupe de France final defeat to Rennes, video caught him appearing to punch an insulting spectator, earning another three-match ban. A summer injury then ruled him out of Brazil's 2019 Copa América triumph. Outside of football, he was hit with a grave allegation. In July 2019, police investigated Neymar for rape. Everything was on the line as the police inquiry unfolded. The payoff finally came when authorities reviewed the case facts. Police announced that the rape investigation against Neymar would be dropped due to insufficient evidence. Controversies and injuries cost him a Ballon d'Or shortlist spot, but the case was over.
