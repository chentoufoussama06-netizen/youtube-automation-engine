Thirty-four camera angles proved Luis Suárez deliberately bit an opponent. Luis Suárez and Uruguay needed a victory against Italy on 24 June 2014 to advance to the World Cup knockout stage. While waiting for a cross, Suárez clashed with defender Giorgio Chiellini. Replays showed Suárez lunged at Chiellini and bit his shoulder, leaving visible bite marks. As Italian players protested to Mexican referee Marco Antonio Rodríguez, Uruguay won a corner and scored, winning 1–0 to eliminate Italy. The FIFA Disciplinary Committee panel studied 34 camera angles. Finding the bite deliberate, FIFA handed Suárez a nine-match international ban and barred him from all football activities for four months.
