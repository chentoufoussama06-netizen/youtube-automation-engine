Seventy-five thousand euros. That is what Karim Benzema was fined. French police had arrested him earlier for his alleged part in blackmailing fellow France international player Mathieu Valbuena over the recording of a sex tape on a mobile phone. The public prosecutor's office announced he would face trial. On 24 November 2021, Benzema was found guilty. The judge gave him a one-year suspended sentence and fined him seventy-five thousand euros. His lawyers said they would appeal his conviction. He was sentenced in this case in June 2022 to a one-year suspended sentence and a fine of sixty-three thousand pounds, after having abandoned the appeal procedure.
