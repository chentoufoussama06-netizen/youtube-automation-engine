Marc-Vivien Foé collapsed on the pitch in 2003, creating a tragic moment that shocked world football. Cameroon faced Colombia in the FIFA Confederations Cup semi-final at the Stade de Gerland in Lyon. A spot in the final was at stake. In the 72nd minute, with Cameroon leading 1–0 after a goal from Pius N'Diefi, defensive midfielder Marc-Vivien Foé suddenly collapsed in the centre circle with no other players near him. Medics rushed to perform mouth-to-mouth resuscitation and spent 45 minutes trying to restart his heart. Tragically, he died shortly afterwards from hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. A beloved star was gone, leaving the entire sport in deep mourning.
