Pep Guardiola received a fourmonth ban for testing positive for nandrolone. After leaving Barcelona in 2001 at age 30, the deep-lying playmaker joined Serie A side Brescia to replace Andrea Pirlo. He played alongside Roberto Baggio before transferring to Roma. However, his time in Italy was unsuccessful. The ultimate low point came when Guardiola was handed a four-month ban for testing positive for nandrolone. It took until 2009, after a protracted legal process, for Guardiola to finally be cleared of all charges.
