Lamine Yamal stepped onto the pitch at 15, the youngest Barcelona starter ever. He delivered two assists, earning Man of the Match and the inaugural U23 Player of the Month. That performance launched a meteoric rise: three consecutive Kopa Trophies, a Ballon d’Or nomination at 17, and a World Cup title by 19. Could any talent eclipse his record?
