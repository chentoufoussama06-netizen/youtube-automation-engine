Mykhailo Mudryk faced a four-year ban from the Premier League. The Ukrainian winger was already serving a suspension after testing positive in a doping test. His number 10 shirt at Chelsea was handed to Cole Palmer, and the FA charged him with anti-doping violations. On 29 April 2026, he was given a ban lasting until 2028. Mudryk appealed against the decision. Then, everything changed. Advances in scientific reporting standards meant his sample would not be reported as positive under updated technical requirements. On 31 July 2026, all charges were dropped. The ban was over.
