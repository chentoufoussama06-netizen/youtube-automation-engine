Parmenion warned Alexander the Great not to cross the Granicus River immediately. The Persian satraps waited on the eastern bank with their field army. Alexander ordered an immediate attack anyway. He engaged the Persians in a hard-fought battle. During the melee, Alexander killed Mithridates and Rhosaces, while Cleitus the Black saved him from Sphithridates. The Macedonians gained the upper hand because their lances were more effective. The Persians were forced onto the defensive.
