The third Roman emperor planned to make his favourite racehorse a consul. Caligula infuriated Rome's nobility by behaving like one of the crowd. As a vocal supporter of the Greens racing team, he personally drove Incitatus, his favourite racehorse. Reports claimed Caligula planned to make the horse a consul, and later, a priest of his own cult. This dark joke turned the highest senatorial ambition into complete ridicule.
