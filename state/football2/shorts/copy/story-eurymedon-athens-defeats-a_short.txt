Athens faced the massive Persian fleet at the mouth of the Eurymedon River. Cimon moved to pre-emptively attack the Persian forces gathered there. Sailing into the river mouth, Cimon quickly routed the Persian fleet. Most of the fleet made landfall, and the sailors fled to the shelter of the Persian army. Cimon landed the Greek marines and attacked the Persian army, which was also routed. The Greeks captured the camp and destroyed 200 triremes.
