A thousand years. That is how long Vercingetorix remained a forgotten name. For centuries, the supreme king of warriors was forgotten throughout the Middle Ages. But in the 16th century, scholars opened Julius Caesar's writings. Humanist study sparked a revival. Scholarly editions and translations of the Gallic Wars brought the ancient leader back to light. After a thousand years of silence, his memory returned. Did Caesar accidentally save the hero he executed?
