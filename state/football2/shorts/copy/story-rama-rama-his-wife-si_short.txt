Fourteen years. That is how long Rama spent exiled in the wilderness. King Dasaratha planned to crown Rama his successor, but stepmother Kaikeyi intervened. She claimed two boons, demanding Rama be exiled into the wilderness for fourteen years while her son Bharata took the throne. Bound by his word, the grief-stricken king acceded. Rama accepted the decree with calm self-control. Sita convinced him to take her along, and his brother Lakshmana resolved to follow. They left Ayodhya to journey southward, building cottages along the Godavari river. Duty forced a prince into the wild.
