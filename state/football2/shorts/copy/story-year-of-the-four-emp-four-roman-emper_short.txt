The Year of the Four Emperors—AD 69—unleashed a whirlwind of betrayal, battles, and bloodshed in just twelve months. Vitellius crushed Otho at the First Battle of Bedriacum on 14 April; Otho took his own life the next day and the Senate named Vitellius emperor on 19 April. Meanwhile, Vespasian, legate of Syria, won the loyalty of Danube and Egyptian legions, was acclaimed in Alexandria on 1 July, and sent Mucianus east while his general Antonius Primus struck the Second Battle of Bedriacum on 24 October, defeating Vitellius. A mob murdered Vitellius on 20 December, and Vespasian secured Rome, ending the chaos and founding the Flavian dynasty. Rome survived, but the empire learned how quickly power could crumble.
