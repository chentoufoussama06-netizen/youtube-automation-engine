Three days. That is how long the outnumbered Greeks held Thermopylae. But how long could they survive before the Persian army broke through? Led by Leonidas, the Greeks blocked the road for three days using the phalanx. Then, a local resident named Ephialtes revealed a mountain path to the Persians. Outflanked, Leonidas dismissed the bulk of the Greek army. The remaining Spartans and Thespians fought to the death. The pass was lost.
