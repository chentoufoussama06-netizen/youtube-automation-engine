Cyrus Drained the Euphrates to Capture Babylon. Cyrus the Great needed a way inside without an armed confrontation. First, his army routed the Babylonian forces at Opis and took Sippar. Then, using a basin dug by Queen Nitokris, the Persians diverted the Euphrates River into a canal. The water dropped to a man's thigh. Persian troops marched right down the dry river bed at night and entered Babylon without resistance.
