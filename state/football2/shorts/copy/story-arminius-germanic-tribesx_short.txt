Arminius, a Germanic chieftain and Roman officer, planned a devastating betrayal in 9 AD. His Roman military education allowed him to methodically deceive General Varus and anticipate tactical responses. Between September 8 and 11, Arminius led an alliance of Germanic peoples in a brutal ambush against three Roman legions in the Teutoburg Forest. This overwhelming Germanic victory sparked a clean sweep of Roman forts and ended Roman expansion into northern Europe.
