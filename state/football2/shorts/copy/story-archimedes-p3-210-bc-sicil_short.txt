With the citadel fallen, the conquest of Syracuse was absolute. Archimedes had died in the siege, but Rome's ultimate prize was now secured. By 210 BC, Rome united the entire island of Sicily into its very first province outside mainland Italy. Denying the Carthaginians a crucial foothold, this victory protected Italy and freed the Romans to focus on Spain. Sicily became the vital springboard for Rome’s ultimate triumph in Africa.
