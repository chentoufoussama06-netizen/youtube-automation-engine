Darius held a massive numerical advantage. Alexander the Great led his outnumbered Macedonian army into the decisive Battle of Gaugamela in 331 BC. Alexander marched his phalanxes forward and pulled Persian forces outward, luring them to the flanks. He formed a giant wedge and smashed into the weakened Persian center. Darius broke and ran. This final blow destroyed the Achaemenid Empire, resulting in its complete conquest by Alexander.
