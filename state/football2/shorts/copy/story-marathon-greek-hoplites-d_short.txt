In 490 BCE, the Battle of Marathon saw Athens and Plataea face the vastly more numerous Persian army. King Darius I sought to subjugate Greece. Athenian general Miltiades ordered a general attack against the Persian forces. He reinforced his flanks, luring the Persians' best fighters into his center. The wheeling flanks enveloped and routed the Persians, who broke in panic towards their ships. This crushing victory ended the first Persian invasion.
