In October 336 BC, King Philip II of Macedon faced a shocking death at his daughter’s wedding. Philip had united Greece and built a formidable army, planning an invasion of the Achaemenid Empire. Gathering at the ancient capital of Aegae for the celebration, Philip entered the town's theatre unprotected to appear approachable. Suddenly, his royal bodyguard, Pausanias of Orestis, approached and stabbed him in the ribs. Philip died instantly, leaving his son Alexander to inherit the kingdom.
