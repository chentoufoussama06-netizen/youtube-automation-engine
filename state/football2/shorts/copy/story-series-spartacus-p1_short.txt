Seventy. That is how many kitchen-armed gladiators Spartacus led to freedom. In 73 BC, Spartacus and about 70 other gladiators had seized kitchen utensils to fight their way out of a gladiatorial school near Capua. Now, the praetor Gaius Claudius Glaber waited for starvation to force their surrender. Instead, Spartacus and his men climbed down the steep side of the volcano using ropes made from vines. They launched a surprise attack on the Roman camp from the rear, killing most of the militia. Soon some 70,000 people had joined him. Part two is next.
