Boudica led an army of one hundred and twenty thousand rebels. She targeted Londinium, intending to burn the young Roman settlement to the ground. Roman governor Gaius Suetonius Paulinus arrived first. Outnumbered, he abandoned Londinium, leaving the rebels to burn the settlement and kill everyone left behind. Up to eighty thousand people died across the revolt. But Suetonius regrouped, crushed Boudica's army in battle, and confirmed Roman control of Britain.
