At Masada, Jewish rebels faced ultimate destruction or impossible surrender. During the First Jewish-Roman War, the Sicarii made their final stand atop Herod's massive desert fortress. Led by Eleazar ben Ya'ir, 960 rebels held off the Roman Tenth Legion. Lucius Flavius Silva built a circumvallation wall and a giant siege ramp. When Romans breached the walls in 73/74 AD, they found nearly 1,000 inhabitants dead by mass suicide. They chose death over Roman chains.
