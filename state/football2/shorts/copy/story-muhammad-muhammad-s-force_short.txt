Muhammad’s Trench Stops a Massive Meccan Assault. Outnumbered in Medina, Muhammad faced an invading force primarily made of merchants and allied tribes. Following the advice of Salman the Persian, every capable Muslim spent six days digging a massive trench along the northern front. When the Quraysh arrived in 627 CE, the unfamiliar defense stopped their movement completely. Violent winds, freezing rain, and starving horses broke the enemy's spirit. After two weeks, the confederates retreated. Medina stood unconquered.
