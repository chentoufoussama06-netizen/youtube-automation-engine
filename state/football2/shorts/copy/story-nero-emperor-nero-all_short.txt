Emperor Nero sang the "Sack of Ilium" in stage costume. Suetonius and Cassius Dio claimed he started the Great Fire of Rome in 64 to clear land for his palatial Golden House. While the city burned, popular legend held that he played the lyre. Some Romans thought the fire an accident, while others claimed arson. Tacitus noted Nero was in Antium when it started, returning to organize relief and open his palaces for survivors.
