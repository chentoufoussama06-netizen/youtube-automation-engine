Ptolemy XIII fled the city after a crushing defeat. Caesar and Cleopatra had won the Battle of the Nile in 47 BC. Roman reinforcements forced the pharaoh to escape. Whether he sought negotiations or tried to flee remains uncertain. On 13 January 47 BC, he attempted to cross the Nile river. He reportedly drowned during the attempt. Cleopatra VII remained the unchallenged ruler of Egypt.
