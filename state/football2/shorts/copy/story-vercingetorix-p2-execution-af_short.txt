After his surrender at Alesia, Vercingetorix spent six years in a Roman cell. Julius Caesar kept the supreme king of warriors imprisoned until his civil war ended. In 46 BC, Caesar finally paraded the Gallic hero through Rome during his delayed triumph. But public hatred demanded revenge. To avoid appearing soft on Rome's enemy, Caesar ordered his execution in the Tullianum, likely by strangulation. Was Caesar right to kill him?
