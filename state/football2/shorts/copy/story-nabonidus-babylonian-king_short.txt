Nabonidus vanished from Babylon, disappearing into the desert oasis of Tayma. What would his ten-year exile mean for the empire’s fragile stability? In May 553 BC he marched into Arabia, crushing a rebellion in the Anti-Lebanon Mountains. By spring he had defeated Dadanu’s king, seized Tayma and declared it his seat. He returned in October 543 BC, leaving Belshazzar to rule and year halted. Did his exile doom Babylon?
