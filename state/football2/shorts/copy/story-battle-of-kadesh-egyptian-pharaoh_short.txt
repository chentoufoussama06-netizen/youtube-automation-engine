Ramses’ Epic Chariot Charge. Leading his army into ancient Syria, Ramesses sought to reclaim the province of Amurru from Muwatalli II of the Hittite Empire. Tricked by Hittite spies, the Pharaoh marched ahead, completely unaware that the enemy was hiding behind Kadesh. Suddenly, Hittite chariots ambushed the Egyptian Ra division, scattering the troops and bursting straight into Ramesses’s camp. Surrounded and deserted, Ramesses called upon his god Amun and personally led a massive, desperate chariot charge through the Hittite ranks with his bodyguard. He broke through the enemy line, driving the plunderers back toward the Orontes River. Reinforced by the arriving Ne'arin troops and the Ptah division, the Egyptians pushed the Hittites into the river.
