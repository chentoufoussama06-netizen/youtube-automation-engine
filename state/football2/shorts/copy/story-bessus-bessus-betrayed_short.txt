As Alexander pursued them, Bessus killed Darius III by the road. The satrap of Bactria conspired with other Persian grandees against Darius III. In mid-330 BC, they arrested their ruler and carried him off in a covered wagon. As Alexander the Great pursued them near Hecatompylus, Bessus killed Darius III and left his body by the road. In the autumn of 330 BC, Bessus declared himself King of Kings, assuming the name Artaxerxes V.
