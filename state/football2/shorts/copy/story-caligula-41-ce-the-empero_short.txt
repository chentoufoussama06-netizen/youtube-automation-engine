Caligula was assassinated just four years into his rule. During games for Divus Augustus, Caligula stopped beneath the palace to address a troupe of young actors. Suddenly, Chaerea, Sabinus, and other conspirators accosted him in the narrow passage. Chaerea struck first, stabbing the emperor before his Germanic guard could reach him. The third Roman emperor was dead, stabbed by his own Praetorian Guard.
