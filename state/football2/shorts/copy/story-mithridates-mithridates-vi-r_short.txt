Mithridates consumed deadly poisons to build an immunity. The king of Pontus feared poisoners after his father was assassinated with arsenic. To protect himself, Mithridates regularly ingested sub-lethal doses of toxins, cultivated antidotes, and tested them on condemned criminals. But when he later tried to take his own life, the deadly poison had no effect. His daily protection prevented the drug from working when he wanted to die.
