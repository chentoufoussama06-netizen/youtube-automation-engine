Three months. That is how long Pertinax lasted before being murdered. When the Praetorian Guard confronted him, he tried to negotiate a peace. After Commodus was assassinated on New Year's Eve 192, Pertinax was named emperor. But his discipline and attempt to initiate reforms aroused immediate opposition in the Guard. He refused to pay their donativum and revoked their privileges. The mutineers plotted his assassination. Trying to reason with them, Pertinax was killed. The Praetorians then auctioned off the throne.
