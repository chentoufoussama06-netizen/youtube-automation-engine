His own mother called Claudius a monster and a standard for stupidity. Drusus died on campaign in Germania from a fall from a horse. Following this tragedy, Claudius was raised by his mother, who never remarried. Later, when his disability became evident, his relationship with his family turned sour. Antonia referred to him as a monster and used him as a standard for stupidity. She passed her son off to his grandmother Livia.
