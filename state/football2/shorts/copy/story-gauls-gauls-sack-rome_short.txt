The Roman army was completely routed by the Gauls at the Allia River. Rome sat defenceless. Led by Brennus, the Senones marched on Rome so quickly that the city was thunderstruck. Finding open gates and unmanned walls, the Gauls entered and sacked the city. Panic gripped the inhabitants. While some defenders fled to Veii, others fortified the Capitoline Hill. After a siege, the Senones were eventually defeated. This Gallic sack left a long-lasting and profound fear of the Gauls in Rome.
