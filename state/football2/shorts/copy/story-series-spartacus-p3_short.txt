Spartacus was presumed killed in battle, but his body vanished. Spartacus tried to negotiate a truce, but Crassus refused. Trapped, the rebels broke through the Roman fortifications and fled toward Brundusium. But discipline collapsed, and isolated groups began attacking the Romans on their own. Seeing no other choice, Spartacus turned his army around in 71 BC, launching his full strength against Crassus’s legions in one final stand. The rebels were utterly defeated and routed on the battlefield. Spartacus was presumed killed in the fight, though his body was never found. Crassus crucified 6,000 captured survivors along the Appian Way.
