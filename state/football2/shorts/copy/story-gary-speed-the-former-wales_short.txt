Gary Speed’s Tragic Suicide Shocks Football. As Wales manager, Speed was the catalyst for a historic national turnaround. He appointed Aaron Ramsey captain, took the team from their lowest-ever ranking of 117th up to 45th, and won his final game 4–1 against Norway. On 26 November 2011, Speed appeared on BBC’s Football Focus in fine form. He watched a match with Alan Shearer, joked with a friend via text, and drove home to Huntington. , his wife Louise found him unresponsive in their garage. She called emergency services, who guided her through resuscitation efforts after advising her to cut the cable with which Speed had hanged himself. Police confirmed his death, which was reported as a suicide. Tributes poured in across the sport, from Leeds United to Newcastle. Speed’s revolutionary work on team culture and facilities paved the way for Wales to finally reach Euro 2016 and the 2022 World Cup.
