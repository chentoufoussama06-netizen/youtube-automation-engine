Themistocles, once Greece’s savior, now faced Sparta’s wrath. His exile teetered as rivals schemed his final ruin. Ostracised in 471 BC, he fled to Argos. Sparta blamed him for the 478 BC Pausanias plot. Escaping south, he found refuge with Alexander I. He then crossed to Asia Minor, serving Artaxerxes I. The king made him governor of Magnesia, where he died. How does a betrayed Athenian rule Persia?
