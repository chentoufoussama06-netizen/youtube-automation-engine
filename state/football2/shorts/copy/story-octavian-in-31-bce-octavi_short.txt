Octavian Wins at Actium, Becomes Emperor. Mark Antony and Cleopatra risked everything to break Octavian’s naval blockade at Actium. Octavian’s fleet of smaller, maneuverable ships routed Antony’s forces. After pursuing them to Alexandria, Octavian consolidated absolute power. The Senate named him Augustus, marking the end of the Roman Republic and the birth of the Roman Empire. Was Octavian Rome’s greatest master?
