After Rome’s disastrous defeat at Carrhae, Marcus Licinius Crassus was killed and twenty thousand Romans died. The brilliant Parthian general Surena had utterly crushed the invading legions using his mobile cavalry. Meanwhile, King Orodes II defeated the Armenians with the rest of the Parthian army and captured their country. But Surena’s massive victory invoked the jealousy of the Parthian king. Orodes II ordered Surena's execution. Ultimate triumph cost the master general his life.
