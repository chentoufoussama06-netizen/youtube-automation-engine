Teenage emperor Elagabalus shocked Rome by committing a crime punishable by being buried alive. The young Syrian priest showed total disregard for Roman traditions. Claiming the union would produce godlike children, the ruler flagrantly breached Roman law by marrying the Vestal Virgin Julia Aquilia Severa. This outrageous act disgusted the Praetorian Guard and the Senate alike. At just eighteen years of age, Elagabalus was assassinated. The empire had its revenge.
