Rome's richest man killed one in ten of his soldiers. Alarmed by the defeats, Rome sent two consular legions, but Spartacus defeated them both. The rebellion now directly threatened the Roman heartland. Terrified, the Senate charged Marcus Licinius Crassus, the wealthiest man in Rome and the only volunteer, with stopping him. Crassus took command of eight legions and enforced ruthless discipline, reviving decimation: slaying one in ten of his own soldiers so they feared him more than the enemy. When Spartacus retreated to Rhegium, Crassus built massive fortifications across the isthmus, trapping the rebels and cutting off all supplies. Then Crassus went after Spartacus himself.
