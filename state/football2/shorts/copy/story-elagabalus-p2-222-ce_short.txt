Elagabalus alienated Rome’s elites, forcing his grandmother to plot against him. His religious provocations and perceptibly foreign conduct drove away the Praetorian Guard, the Senate, and common people alike. Seeing popular support vanish, his grandmother Julia Maesa realized he and his mother had to be replaced. She targeted her other daughter and grandson, Severus Alexander. Maesa devised an assassination plot, executed by disaffected Praetorian Guard members in March 222. At just 18 years of age, Elagabalus was assassinated and replaced by his cousin. The teenage emperor’s reign ended in blood.
