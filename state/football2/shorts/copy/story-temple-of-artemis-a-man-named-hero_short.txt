356 BC. That is when Herostratus burned the Temple of Artemis. So in 356 BC, he set fire to the wooden roof-beams of the Temple of Artemis, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. As the grand sanctuary in Ephesus burned to the ground, the citizens sentenced the arsonist to death. They strictly forbade anyone from ever mentioning his name again. But his crime survived history anyway.
