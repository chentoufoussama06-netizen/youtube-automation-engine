Hatshepsut Poses as a Male Pharaoh to Seize Power. What price did she pay for wearing a false beard? She rose from regent for toddler Thutmose III to claim the throne by Year 7. Depicted with a beard and ram’s horns, she ruled Egypt peacefully, building Karnak’s twin obelisks and her mortuary temple. After twenty years, Thutmose III erased her monuments, trying to wipe her reign. Who truly ruled?
