Four legions surrounded Jerusalem. In 70 CE, Titus led at least 48,200 Roman soldiers into Judaea to crush the First Jewish Revolt. Inside the capital, rival factions fought each other and destroyed food supplies. The Romans breached the walls and captured the Temple Mount. They destroyed the Second Temple, razed the city, and killed, enslaved, or displaced much of the population. The revolt was over.
