Rome burned while Emperor Nero reportedly sang the Sack of Ilium. Many Romans believed the emperor instigated the Great Fire to clear land for his planned Golden House. Suetonius and Cassius Dio claim Nero performed in stage costume while the city burned. To remove suspicion from himself, Nero seized Christians as scapegoats. Tacitus writes that Nero had them thrown to beasts, crucified, and burned alive out of personal cruelty.
