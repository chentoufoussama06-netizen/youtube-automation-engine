Cyrus Lets the Jews Return to Jerusalem. Cyrus the Great issued the Edict of Restoration following the Persian conquest of Babylon. This freed the Jewish people and enabled the return to Zion, repatriating them to the Kingdom of Judah. Cyrus facilitated Jewish aspirations for a new Temple in Jerusalem, resulting in the completion of the Second Temple. The exiles returned to their land, and ancient sovereignty was restored.
