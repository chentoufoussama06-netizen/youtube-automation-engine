Themistocles saved Greece by tricking the Persian king into a deadly trap. In 483 BC, this Athenian politician persuaded Athens to build 200 triremes using silver from the Laurion mines. When Xerxes invaded in 480 BC, Themistocles commanded the Athenian navy. Through clever subterfuge and a fake message, he lured the Persian fleet into the cramped Straits of Salamis. The decisive Greek victory became the turning point of the war.
