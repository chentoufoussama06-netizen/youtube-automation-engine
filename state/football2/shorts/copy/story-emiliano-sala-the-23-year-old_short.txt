Emiliano Sala Killed in Plane Crash. The twenty-eight-year-old Argentine striker rose to fame at French club Nantes, finishing as their top goalscorer for three consecutive seasons. In January 2019, Cardiff City signed Sala for a club record fee of fifteen million pounds. Eager to play in the Premier League, Sala even rejected a higher salary from a Chinese Super League club. After completing his medical, Sala returned to France to say goodbye to his teammates. On January 21, 2019, he boarded a Piper Malibu light aircraft to fly back to Cardiff. Mid-flight, the plane carrying Sala disappeared over the English Channel. An initial three-day search covering four thousand four hundred square kilometres failed to locate the site. After global calls to resume the effort, private searches commenced. On February 3, the wreckage was discovered on the seabed. Sala’s body was recovered four days later, with fingerprint evidence confirming his identity. Nantes retired his number nine jersey to honor the beloved striker. A young life and a promising career were cut devastatingly short.
