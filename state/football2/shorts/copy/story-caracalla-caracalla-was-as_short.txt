217. That is when Roman Emperor Caracalla was stabbed while urinating. In 217, Caracalla was travelling on the road from Edessa to Carrhae to visit a temple of the moon god Sin. Stopping briefly to urinate, he was approached by a Roman soldier, Justin Martialis, and stabbed. A bodyguard killed Martialis with his lance, and two Praetorian tribunes completed the assassination. Macrinus succeeded him as emperor three days later.
