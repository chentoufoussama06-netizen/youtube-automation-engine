Agrippina the Younger boarded a boat designed to sink her in AD 59. Nero wanted her dead after she fell out of favor. Anicetus advised building a collapsible vessel. A collapsing lead ceiling crushed her attendant. The boat failed to sink, so the crew sank it. Agrippina swam to shore. News of her survival reached Nero. He sent men to kill her, making it look like suicide.
