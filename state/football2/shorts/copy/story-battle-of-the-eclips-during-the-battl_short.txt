The Battle of the Eclipse brought a sudden halt to a long-standing war in Anatolia. For five years, the Lydians and Medes fought with various success, each gaining many victories over the other. In the sixth year, another combat took place. Just as the battle was growing warm, day was on a sudden changed into night. Observing the change, both armies ceased fighting and agreed on peace terms. Daylight was lost, but peace was won.
