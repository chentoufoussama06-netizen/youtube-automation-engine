Why Brutus Turned on Caesar. These conspirators, led by Marcus Brutus and Gaius Cassius Longinus, aimed to destroy Caesar's perpetual dictatorship and save the Roman Republic. Inside the Curia of Pompey, Tillius Cimber approached Caesar with a petition and suddenly grabbed his toga. Caesar cried out, "Why, this is violence!" Publius Servilius Casca struck first, aiming for Caesar's neck. Caesar blocked him, but Casca screamed for his brother, who stabbed Caesar in the side. The conspirators swarmed, striking him from all directions with daggers. Blind by his own blood, Caesar fell beside a statue of Pompey and died from twenty-three stab wounds. Their bloody strike killed Caesar, but it destroyed the Republic forever.
