Ravana's own brother turned renegade and joined the Ramayana battle. Rama and Lakshmana proceeded with their allies towards the shore of the southern sea. They were joined by Ravana's renegade brother Vibhishana. Then, the vānaras named Nala and Nila constructed the Rama Setu. The princes and their army crossed over to Lanka. A lengthy war ensued against the army of Ravana.
