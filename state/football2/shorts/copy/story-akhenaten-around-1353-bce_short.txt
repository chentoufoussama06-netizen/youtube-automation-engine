Pharaoh Akhenaten left Thebes behind. He abandoned a powerful priesthood to build something entirely new. Around 1353 BCE, he decreed that a fresh site be constructed on the east bank of the Nile. He called the new capital city Akhetaten, better known today as Amarna. Only his most loyal subjects followed him into the desert. He broke with Amun.
