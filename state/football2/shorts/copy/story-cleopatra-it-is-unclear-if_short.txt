Thirty-nine years old. That is all the time Cleopatra had. She prepared for suicide after learning Octavian planned to move her and her children to Rome. Her servants Eiras and Charmion apparently accompanied her and also took their own lives. Yet history left one final mystery unresolved. It remains unclear if her suicide on 12 August 30 BC took place within the palace or her tomb.
