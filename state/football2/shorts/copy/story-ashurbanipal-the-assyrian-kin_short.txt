During his 38-year reign, Ashurbanipal built the world's first organized library. Using his massive resources, the Neo-Assyrian ruler created the world’s first systematically organized library in Nineveh. Scribes scoured the empire to collect and copy over 100,000 texts on clay tablets. These documents were separated by subject and organized by shape into different rooms. Ashurbanipal considered this massive collection to be the signature accomplishment of his 38-year reign.
