April 1204, the Fourth Crusade stormed Constantinople’s walls. What would become of its priceless relics and citizens? On 9 April the Crusaders assaulted the Golden Horn, but rain and arrows forced them back. On 12 April a north wind helped Venetians seize towers; seventy knights slipped through breach. They pillaged days, melting bronze Hercules and Hippodrome horses, killing 2,000 and installing Baldwin emperor. Who bore the greatest blame?
