2334 BC. That is when Sargon forged the world's first imperial experiment. Sargon suddenly overturned the established order, seizing the north. Then, he confronted and defeated Lugal-zage-si, the king of Uruk, placing the southern land of Sumer under Akkadian control. Around 2334 BC, Sargon forged what is generally considered the world's first imperial experiment. He became the archetype of the conquering king.
