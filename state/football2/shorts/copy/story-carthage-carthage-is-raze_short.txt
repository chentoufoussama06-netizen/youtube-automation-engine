Carthage was a powerful Punic empire before Rome reduced it to rubble. Scipio Aemilianus led the final siege that ended Carthaginian power forever. Romans dragged Phoenician warships into the harbor, burned them, and went from house to house capturing the people. About 50,000 Carthaginians were sold into slavery. The city was set ablaze and razed, leaving only ruins and rubble. Rome annexed the majority of the colonies. A brutal peace treaty.
