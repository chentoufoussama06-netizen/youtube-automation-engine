On 24 August 410 AD, the unthinkable happened when the Visigoths brutally sacked Rome. Led by their king, Alaric, the invaders breached the Salarian Gate of the eternal city. Rome, the spiritual center of the Western Roman Empire, faced a foreign fall for the first time in almost 800 years. For three days, Alaric’s forces pillaged the streets, claiming vast wealth and taking high-profile hostages. The world stood deeply shocked.
