Arminius, a trusted Germanic officer, secretly plotted the ultimate betrayal against three Roman legions. In 9 CE, Roman commander Publius Quinctilius Varus marched his forces into the dense Teutoburg Forest. Varus believed Arminius was his loyal ally. In reality, Arminius had received Roman citizenship and a military education, using this knowledge to methodically deceive the Romans and anticipate their tactical responses. By setting up a summer camp deep in Cherusci lands, Arminius lured the Romans into a trap. Between September 8 and 11, the Germanic alliance launched a devastating ambush on the marching columns. The Romans could not breach a pre-constructed zig-zagging wall of peat turves and packed sand. The alliance annihilated three legions, and the Roman Empire lost its grip on the region east of the Rhine.
